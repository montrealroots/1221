# Round-Ups, Bank By Bank: The Research Of 2026-10-13

works. Every bank SLATE has was researched from its own pages, terms and fee schedules, each group of banks by its own researcher,
and each finding was read again by a second researcher, who checked the quotes, dates and engine claims and researched
what the first missed. `findings-2026-10-13.json` holds every finding whole: eligibility, opting in, the purchases
that count and those that don't, the rounding, the source and destination accounts, timing, a short account, refunds,
closure, what the sources leave open, each source's quote and effective date, and what SLATE said before.
`second-reading-2026-10-13.json` holds what the second reading corrected or added.

What SLATE plays from it is `src/engine/content/roundups.ts`: twenty banks, each by its own way, with what its
sources leave open and what SLATE leaves out on its panel. Two banks were decided against with a reason, in the last
