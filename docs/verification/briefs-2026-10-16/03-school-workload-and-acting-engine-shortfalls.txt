1. Investigate The School Workload Problem

The latest report found:

* The London profile was dismissed on all four tested seeds despite 100% attendance.
* From the third term, 24–26 of its 46 class pieces per term were unready.
* Other attentive profiles also had many unready scenes.
* The London outcome reproduced before the latest genre changes.

Determine the cause before changing the balance.

Trace:
Assigned Work → Notice And Deadlines → Available Time → Preparation Options → Preparation Progress → Class Performance → Feedback → Academic Standing → Intervention → Final Outcome.

Distinguish:

* A scheduling or engine defect.
* An unrealistic workload.
* A weak automated play policy.
* Poor player choices.
* Legitimate consequences of low skill or incomplete preparation.
* Misleading readiness labels or forecasts.

Attendance alone must not guarantee passing. Equally, a reasonable, attentive strategy must not fail because of hidden requirements, impossible scheduling, ineffective controls, or preparation that the game fails to credit.

2. Establish A Feasible Student Schedule

* Required classes and rehearsals.
* Assigned preparation and memorization.
* Performances and assessments.
* Work-study obligations where applicable.
* Sleep, recovery, meals, and other necessary existing actions.
* Illness, leave, and reasonable disruption.

Account for overlapping obligations, advance notice, deadlines, and the opportunity to prepare before the relevant class.

Use authoritative school information where factual requirements matter. Distinguish researched requirements from SLATE’s designed time costs.

Demonstrate at least one feasible, understandable strategy for representative starting profiles. Do not rely on hidden knowledge, impossible action sequences, or eliminating recovery to make the schedule work.

Fix the actual cause. Do not simply lower pass thresholds, raise starting attributes, grant free preparation, or remove consequences.

3. Make Preparation And Feedback Consistent

Verify that:

* Relevant preparation reaches the correct piece and assessment.
* Repeated work earns the intended progress without duplication.
* Practice duration, diminishing returns, and completion marks agree.
* Changing an interpretation preserves applicable work and clearly explains any new preparation needed.
* Readiness labels accurately describe what remains.
* Advice prioritizes urgent work appropriately.
* Players receive actionable warnings early enough to respond.
* Leave, repeated terms, extra terms, and reloads preserve legitimate progress.

Make the consequences understandable before the player is penalized. A warning should identify the problem, the available remedy, and the relevant deadline.

Keep school challenge meaningful. Neglectful strategies should still carry appropriate risks.
