4. Stamina: Implement Duration And Intensity

I authorize performance workload to reflect:

* The production’s running time.
* The actor’s actual participation.
* Physical and vocal intensity where represented.
* Breaks and recovery.
* Starting condition and stamina.

Stop treating short one-act productions automatically as full evenings.

Use production-specific running times where supported. A play does not have one universally exact runtime; label estimates and make their basis clear.

Design a bounded, understandable model that:

* Gives longer or more demanding participation greater workload when other inputs are equal.
* Allows a short intense performance to be demanding.
* Gives stamina meaningful protective value without making it unlimited energy.
* Accounts for recovery without double-counting it.
* Applies elapsed time and fatigue once.
* Handles segmented performances consistently with the same performance processed as a whole.
* Explains the workload and consequences to the player.

Numerical coefficients are SLATE design choices unless genuinely supported by research. Document and balance them through testing rather than claiming an exact scientific formula for acting performance.

Update forecasts, tutorials, saved results, and migrations as necessary. Preserve historical outcomes under their original ruleset.
