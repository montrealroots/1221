SLATE: Complete Genre Differentiation And Prove Each Genre’s Identity

Focus exclusively on genre differentiation. Continue from the latest code, preserve other ongoing work, and do not reopen banking or unrelated acceptance tasks.

Read the repository instructions, current work plan, genre matrix, phase-2b report, existing briefs, evaluation rules, and genre acceptance criteria before editing.

1. Resolve The Seven Identical Genre Pairs

The last reviewed report identified these mechanically identical pairs:

* Biker / Coming Of Age
* Children’s / Science Fantasy
* Christmas / Holiday
* Dystopian / Medical Drama
* Espionage / Neorealism
* Family / Steampunk
* Nordic Noir / Social Realism

Recheck them against the current implementation. Identify exactly which engine-consumed values and choices are identical, then give each genre an appropriate, demonstrated identity.

Do not differentiate them by arbitrary numerical offsets or different descriptions over identical behavior.

2. Review Every Genre

Inspect every genre in the actual catalog—not only these seven pairs.

For each, document:

* Its defining conventions and dramatic concerns.
* Relevant performance demands.
* Interpretation choices and their tradeoffs.
* What preparation helps and why.
* How its scenes vary internally.
* Which engine calculations read those distinctions.
* How the player sees and understands the consequences.

Recognize that the catalog may mix genres, settings, audiences, movements, and seasonal categories. A setting label does not automatically require a unique acting technique. Where two categories legitimately share performance demands, differentiate their situations, constraints, interpretation opportunities, and feedback without inventing false acting rules.

3. Implement Meaningful Differences

Use the existing scene and evaluation systems wherever possible. Distinctions may involve:

* Stakes and objectives.
* Subtext and explicitness.
* Emotional restraint or intensity.
* Rhythm, timing, and tonal transitions.
* Physical or vocal demands.
* Relationship dynamics and partner cues.
* Text complexity and continuity.
* Acceptable interpretation choices.

Ensure these properties affect preparation, rehearsal, evaluation, or specific feedback where appropriate.

Avoid one rigid formula for every scene in a genre. Give genres recognizable tendencies while allowing different roles, scenes, and valid interpretations.

Add or revise briefs where the current content cannot demonstrate sufficient range. Do not use a large content count as a substitute for depth.

4. Preserve Attribute Integration And Mastery

Use existing attributes and genre proficiency coherently. Keep this work focused on genre differentiation; change shared evaluation code only where necessary for that goal.

Preserve:

* Relevant genre learning improving relevant work.
* Unrelated genre mastery providing no unjustified universal bonus.
* The established maximum-personal-quality guarantee under qualifying mastery conditions.
* Individual performance being distinct from ensemble results.
* Existing saved results and evaluation-version compatibility.

Do not differentiate genres by making some permanently incapable of maximum quality.

5. Make Consequences Visible

The player should understand why approaching two genres differently matters.

Update relevant brief descriptions, rehearsal feedback, genre explanations, and contextual tutorials. Explain actual mechanics and choices without exposing unnecessary developer terminology.

Feedback must reflect the evaluation evidence. Remember prior rehearsal decisions and avoid repeating resolved advice.

Follow existing Title Case and interface conventions.

6. Prove The Differences

Keep the existing acceptance thresholds fixed unless a documented flaw requires correction. Do not loosen them merely to obtain passing results.

Provide:

* A before-and-after matrix covering every genre.
* A specific demonstration for each formerly identical pair.
* Controlled comparisons using the same actor, proficiency, condition, and preparation.
* Examples where genre-appropriate choices affect the relevant outcome.
* Examples showing multiple interpretations can succeed.
* Low, medium, high, and mastered proficiency checks.
* Tests preventing unintended effects on unrelated genres.
* Save/load checks and preservation of historical results.

Compare genre behavior across varied scenes, rather than relying on one carefully selected brief. Distinguish legitimate overlap from accidental duplication.

Use targeted mutation tests to show that removing the new genre distinctions breaks the relevant tests.

7. Verify The Player Experience

Play representative journeys through selecting a brief, preparing, rehearsing, interpreting, performing, receiving feedback, and continuing after a reload.

Cover the formerly identical pairs and representative contrasting genre families. Include desktop and mobile checks for affected interactions.

Measure repeated content and feedback during repeated practice. Improve meaningful variation where the same choices and advice recur despite changed circumstances.

Do not claim infinite unique content or that automated tests prove subjective enjoyment.

8. Work Efficiently And Finish

Reuse existing research, infrastructure, and completed evidence. Research only unresolved genre-specific questions, using appropriate authoritative sources and distinguishing acting principles from SLATE’s designed formulas.

Avoid duplicated agent assignments and repeated repository-wide investigation. Run focused tests while implementing, then the required full regression gate and build on the final integrated revision.

Continue until every genre-differentiation acceptance item is implemented and demonstrated, or a concrete blocker prevents further progress. Do not stop after producing the matrix or a plan.

Deliver:

* The implementation commit and branch.
* Exactly what changed for each formerly identical pair.
* The complete genre matrix.
* Before-and-after gameplay examples.
* Test commands, results, and mutation evidence.
* Remaining limitations or genuine unresolved decisions.
* Updated tutorials and handoff.

Commit and push the completed work and prepare a reviewable pull request. Do not merge or deploy without explicit authorization.

The goal is recognizable, varied, mechanically meaningful genres that work naturally with the attribute engine—with proof, not cosmetic labels.
