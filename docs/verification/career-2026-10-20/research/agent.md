## Why Parente

- **Department:** A Yale MFA actor starting in New York needs one agent who covers both stage and screen. His published scope, "Television, Film, Broadway, Off-Broadway, National Tours and Regional Theatre", fits that more closely than any other candidate's. The colleague who leads Stewart's film and TV side is the alternate below.
- **Market and agency size:** Stewart is a mid-size agency, with a theatrical department and four offices. The bigger agencies a Yale graduate might sign with (Gersh, Innovative Artists, Buchwald, CAA, WME, UTA) don't name their individual agents on their own sites, so none of them can meet the "agency's own page first" standard. Stewart does name its agents.
- **Stability:** He has been at the agency since 2015.
- **How current the evidence is:** Two 2025 sources (Deadline, 2025-09-18; Actors Connection, 2025-06-02), plus a staff list updated after September 2025 and read today. I found no 2026 source that names him.
- **Path into the game:** The agency takes no unsolicited legit submissions, so meeting him should come through a referral or the Yale showcase, not a cold submission. That matches how the agency actually works. The Yale showcase page (https://www.drama.yale.edu/training/acting/actor-showcase-25/, heading "2025 Actor Showcase", footer "Copyright © 2026") lists the New York showcase at La MaMa on April 28–29. It doesn't say who is invited, so **whether Stewart Talent or Parente attends is unverified**. The game must not claim that he does.

## What the game may use, and what it must not

**May use** (published, cited above): the name "Jordan Parente"; "Stewart Talent"; "New York office"; "Agent, Theatrical (Legit) Department"; that he represents actors for film, TV and theatre; and the policy "does not accept unsolicited legit submissions".

**Must not use:** any email address, phone number or street address; clients, real or implied; commission or contract terms (none published); his tastes, temperament, opinions or way of speaking (none published, so anything would be invented but read as real); the seminar listing's "always looking for…" line as his words; any claim that he saw a showcase, tape or player; any union-franchise claim (unverified); his education.

## Risks, and how the game should label it

1. **Invented acts by a real, named person.** Any acceptance, rejection or remark the game attributes to Parente could be read as true, or as putting him in a false light. A "fictional" label reduces this risk but doesn't remove it. The safest course is for the owner to get written permission from Stewart Talent before release, and to keep a fictional agent ready as a fallback. This is a risk flag, not legal advice.
2. **Impersonation scams.** The agency's homepage warns about people posing as its representatives to approach unrepresented actors, which is exactly the situation the game would show. So the game should never show a real-looking email, DM or phone call from him, never use a stewarttalent.com address or any real contact detail, and never show him seeking players out. Contact should run through referral or showcase scenes.
3. **Thin public profile.** About two sentences about him are public. Keep him procedural and neutral: decisions shown as outcomes, not as his personality.
4. **The record can change.** Re-check contact_new_york before each release, and make the name swappable (alternate 1 needs only a name and department change).
5. **The real policy and play.** The game must not suggest that real actors can submit to him, or that he attends the Yale showcase (unverified).

**Labelling:** a notice where he first appears, which the player can open again later:
"Jordan Parente is a real agent at Stewart Talent in New York. In SLATE, everything he says and decides is invented. He hasn't seen your work and isn't involved with this game. Stewart Talent's New York legit department doesn't accept unsolicited submissions, so please don't contact the agency about the game."
Also mark each of his lines or decisions as fiction (for example "Fictional" on his panel), and put the same notice in the credits. The wording still has to pass the project's player-text rules (`docs/STYLE.md`).
