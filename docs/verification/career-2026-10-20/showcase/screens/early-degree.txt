Finishing Early
 Graduating early isn’t a route the game offers. The Bulletin says that “on rare occasions the faculty of David Geffen School of Drama may reduce the residency requirement, but in no case below a minimum of two years,” and publishes no criteria for it and no way to ask. So the MFA comes at Commencement after your sixth term.

The Way You’re On
 Completing The Degree
 Year one of the MFA: the degree comes at Commencement after your sixth term, and everything on your record goes on with you. Graduating early isn’t a route the game offers. The Bulletin says that “on rare occasions the faculty of David Geffen School of Drama may reduce the residency requirement, but in no case below a minimum of two years,” and publishes no criteria for it and no way to ask.
