Your Professional Profile
Show Less

Your Profile is what a submission says about you: the name you work under, your training, your School credits and professional credits kept apart, your special skills, when you’re free, where you’re based and whether you can work in the US.

You set one thing here: your professional name. Everything else is read from your record.

Where To Find It
Career, Portfolio, Your Profile.
When You Can Use It
From the guide’s chapter “Your Career”.
Show Me Where
What To Press
Open Career, then the Portfolio tab.
Under Professional Name, type the name you want to work under.
Press Use This Name.
To go back to your own name, leave the field blank, or type your own name, and press Use This Name.
What You Need First
A name of letters, with spaces, hyphens, apostrophes and periods between them, 40 characters at most.
TIME AND MONEY
Time
None
Money
None
What Happens
STRAIGHT AWAY
Your profile shows the new name at once.
LATER ON
A résumé made under your old name reads as behind, and Update Your Résumé makes one under the new name.
READING THE RESULT
Professional credits are paid work and student films, kept for good. Practice, practice briefs and self-tapes are never credits.
A special skill marked mastered is at its technique’s top, where no piece asks more of it.
How It Connects
CHANGES STRAIGHT AWAY
Your Résumé: Your résumé uses your professional name, and one made under another name reads as behind.
KEPT IN YOUR RECORD
The Player Profile: Your special skills are your techniques on the Player Profile, at the level it shows.
What To Do Next
Make your résumé, or update it, so it carries the name.
If You Can’t
That’s already your professional name.
You’re already working under your own name.
A professional name is 40 characters at most.
MISTAKES, AND PUTTING THEM RIGHT
Changing your name after making your résumé, and sending the old one. Update it after a change.
What Decides It
School Credits
The parts you played at the School once each run has closed, as your Player Profile’s School Journey lists them
Special Skills
A technique at 60 or more among accents and dialects, stage combat, singing, dance, improvisation, clowning, voice-over work, classical text work, comic timing, intimacy work and on-camera work (estimated)
A Worked Example

Ada Cole would rather work as Ada Lynn Cole.

Type Ada Lynn Cole under Professional Name.
Press Use This Name.

What comes of it. Your Profile says you work as Ada Lynn Cole, and a résumé made earlier reads as behind until it’s updated.
