The Actor Showcase
Show Less

Your last term ends with the Actor Showcase (DRAM 793b): two scenes you choose, played in New York on the Monday and Tuesday after classes end, and in Los Angeles the Tuesday after. The Bulletin says the showcase is presented to agents, managers and casting directors.

You choose your scenes from the faculty’s options, prepare them as you prepare any piece, and play them at each showcase day. Each day judges both scenes, and the record keeps how each one went.

Where To Find It
School, Coursework, The Actor Showcase.
When You Can Use It
In your last spring term, from its first day, once you’ve read the guide’s chapter “Drama School”.
Show Me Where
What To Press
In your last term, open School, then the Coursework tab.
Under The Actor Showcase, read the faculty’s note on each scene: its genre, its forecast fully prepared, and any part you’ve played in it before.
Tick two scenes, each in one of its parts, and press Choose These Scenes.
Prepare each on its card below, Actor Showcase: Your Showcase Scene, with its steps, its Table Work and your partner.
Go to the rehearsal calls in the last two weeks of classes, and to each showcase day when the clock stops at it.
What You Need First
To be in your last spring term, enrolled in it: not on leave, and not in an additional term.
Every Incomplete made up by the end of the term’s fourth week, its Friday at five. One still open then keeps you out.
Two different scenes from the options, each in one of its parts.
TIME AND MONEY
Time
None to choose. Each rehearsal call takes three hours; New York’s days ten hours and eight hours, and Los Angeles’s a day and a night, each with its trip (estimated)
Money
None: the School takes you
What Happens
STRAIGHT AWAY
Your two scenes become pieces of Actor Showcase’s coursework, due in New York, each with a partner from your class.
LATER ON
At the end of the term’s fourth week, the School reads your Incompletes. With none, you’re in, and the faculty choose two scenes for you if you haven’t.
At the first showcase day, each scene is shown and gets the faculty’s note. It’s played again the next day, and in Los Angeles it’s shown again with the note taken.
Actor Showcase is settled after Los Angeles, not at the term review. A course with half its pieces not shown isn’t passed, and the degree needs every course passed.
READING THE RESULT
A forecast is what a scene would come to fully prepared on a good day. It’s a forecast, not a result.
Each day’s band is how that scene came out that day: Unready, Working, Strong or Exceptional, with its score of 100.
A showcase day missed counts as a missed call and an unexcused absence from Actor Showcase, and your scenes aren’t seen there. Ill, you’re excused.
How It Connects
COMES FIRST
Repeating, Extra Terms And Incompletes: An Incomplete still open at the end of the term’s fourth week keeps you out of the showcase.
CHANGES STRAIGHT AWAY
Preparing Coursework: Your two scenes become pieces of coursework, each prepared with its own steps.
COUNTS TOWARD
Feedback And Your Notes: Each scene’s note comes at its first showcase day, and Los Angeles shows whether you took it.
KEPT IN YOUR RECORD
The Career Screen: How each scene went at each showcase day stays on your professional record.
What To Do Next
Prepare both scenes on their cards, and answer your partners when they ask to rehearse.
If You Can’t
The Actor Showcase is in your last spring term.
You can’t take part in the Actor Showcase while a course you were given an Incomplete in was still incomplete at the end of the term’s fourth week.
Choose two scenes, and two different ones.
Your scenes are already chosen.
MISTAKES, AND PUTTING THEM RIGHT
Choosing without reading the notes. A scene whose forecast is low for you stays low, however well you prepare it.
Leaving an Incomplete for later. Make it up by the end of the term’s fourth week, or you’re out of the showcase.
What Decides It
The Faculty’s Options
Four scenes, each in either part, ranked by how each would come out for you fully prepared, then by the genres you know and the scenes you’ve done well with (estimated)
Each Showcase Day
Each scene’s preparation, how you are that day, and what the scene asks
Taking Part
Your course records: any course still Incomplete at the end of the term’s fourth week
A Worked Example

Ada Cole is in her last spring term, with no Incomplete outstanding.

Under The Actor Showcase, tick Nina in The Seagull and Gwendolen in The Importance of Being Earnest.
Press Choose These Scenes.
Prepare both on their cards, and go to the rehearsal calls and each showcase day.

What comes of it. Both scenes are judged in New York on the Monday and Tuesday after classes end and in Los Angeles the Tuesday after, and the showcase’s record keeps each day’s band.
