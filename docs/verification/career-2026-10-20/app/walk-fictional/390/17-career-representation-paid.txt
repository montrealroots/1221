The Agreement
How This Works

You signed it on May 30, 2029. It runs until May 30, 2030.

What It Covers (The Game’s Own Term)
Stage, film and television work: the fields the agent’s department covers.
Where (The Game’s Own Term)
Work anywhere in the United States.
What It Leaves Out (The Game’s Own Term)
Commercials and voice-over work aren’t covered. An agency usually handles them in another department.
Exclusive (The Game’s Own Term)
The agent is your only agent for covered work. Equity’s standard form is written the same way for theater.
Commission (A Published Rule)
Ten percent of your gross pay for covered work, and five percent on an Equity contract paying $525 a week or less. The Equity minimum part of per diem or expense money carries no commission.
Scale Plus Ten (A Published Rule)
On SAG-AFTRA work at the minimum, the agent asks for scale plus ten percent, so the commission comes out of the extra ten.
How Long (The Game’s Own Term)
One year from the day you sign it.
What The Agent Does (A Published Rule)
Looks for and submits you for covered work, passes on every offer in writing, negotiates your terms, and collects your pay into the agency’s client trust account.
What You Do (The Game’s Own Term)
Tell the agent when you’re available, keep the appointments made for you, and don’t take covered work without them.
How Pay Reaches You (The Game’s Own Term)
Pay for covered work goes to the agency’s client trust account, and comes on to you within three business days, less commission.
Ending It (A Published Rule)
Either of you can end it by agreement at any time. You can end it if 90 days pass with no real offer of covered work, or, after 151 days, if you’ve had fewer than ten days of covered work in the last 91. Otherwise it ends when the year is up.
What You Still Owe After It Ends (A Published Rule)
Commission stays owed on contracts you made while it ran, and on their renewals, whoever represents you when you’re paid. An offer the agent passed on in the term that you accept within 60 days after it ends is owed too.
How Long The Offer Stands (The Game’s Own Term)
Fourteen days. After that it lapses.
