Your Reel
Show Less

A reel is short recorded work that shows you on camera. It’s cut only from what was recorded: your self-tapes of practice briefs, the School’s filmed monologues once they’re posted at the end of your last term, and screen work once it’s released. An editor cuts up to four pieces in two hours for about $250.00, both estimated.

Where To Find It
Career, Portfolio, Reel.
When You Can Use It
From the guide’s chapter “Your Career”.
Show Me Where
What To Press
Open Career, then the Portfolio tab.
Under Recorded Work To Cut From, tick up to four pieces. Each shows how it was judged and when.
Press Cut A Reel.
What You Need First
Something recorded: tape a practice brief on the Player Profile first.
Free for two hours.
Enough in your bank for the edit.
TIME AND MONEY
Time
two hours (estimated)
Money
$250.00 (estimated)
What Happens
STRAIGHT AWAY
The clock moves on two hours, the edit comes out of your bank, and the reel shows what’s on it, a line a piece.
LATER ON
New recorded work after it shows under What’s Missing as worth adding.
READING THE RESULT
A reel of self-tapes only says so: filmed School work or screen work shows more.
A night on stage and a class piece aren’t recorded, so they never appear here.
How It Connects
COMES FIRST
Taping A Brief: A self-tape of a practice brief is the first recorded work a reel can be cut from.
CHANGES STRAIGHT AWAY
The Career Screen: Once you have a reel, What’s Missing stops asking for one, and says when newer work came after it.
WHAT LEADS HERE
Why It Went This Way: Your reel’s best band is part of Your Work, and a reel is part of Stage And Screen.
What To Do Next
Check What’s Missing on the Overview.
If You Can’t
You have nothing recorded to cut a reel from.
A reel holds four pieces at most.
A voice brief is a sound recording with no picture, so it can’t go on a reel.
Your reel already holds exactly these pieces.
MISTAKES, AND PUTTING THEM RIGHT
Expecting a production’s nights on it. Only recorded work can be.
What Decides It
Self-Tapes
The tapes of each brief you keep, with their bands
Filmed Monologues
Taming The Cyclops passed in your last term: the School posts them at the end of it, as its Bulletin says
A Worked Example

Three self-tapes of camera briefs taped, one of them Strong.

Tick the Strong tape and one other.
Press Cut A Reel.

What comes of it. Two hours on, $250.00 comes out of your bank, and Reel lists the two pieces with their bands. What’s Missing says it holds only self-tapes.
