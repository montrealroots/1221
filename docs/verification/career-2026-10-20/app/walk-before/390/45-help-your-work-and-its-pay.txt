Your Work And Its Pay
Show Less

The Work tab lists each engagement you’ve booked: who it’s for, where, its dates, its calls on your calendar, what your permission to work said for its dates, and its pay, a payment for each week’s work.

Pay is promised when you accept the work, and reaches your accounts only when it’s paid, on the Thursday after each week’s work. Represented, it goes to your agency’s client trust account first and comes on to you within three business days, less commission. Unrepresented, it comes straight to you.

Where To Find It
Career, Work, Your Engagements.
When You Can Use It
From the guide’s chapter “Your Career”. The Work tab lists work once you’ve booked some.
Show Me Where
What To Press
Open Career, then the Work tab.
Under Your Engagements, read each payment: what’s promised, by whom, and when it’s due.
After its payday, the payment says what was withheld for tax, any union dues, the commission and what reached you.
Open Money to see it land, with its line on your account’s statement.
What You Need First
Work you’ve booked. A student film’s pay is deferred, so it’s listed and never paid.
TIME AND MONEY
Tax
Withheld from each payment as a paycheck’s is: federal income tax, Social Security and Medicare, and the tax of the state the work is in
When It’s Paid
The Thursday after each week’s work, as SAG-AFTRA’s rules give it, for all professional work (estimated)
Through Your Agency
Within three business days of the payday, as SAG-AFTRA’s rules give it for TV and film, for stage work too (estimated)
What Happens
STRAIGHT AWAY
Accepting work puts each payment on the Work tab as promised. Your accounts don’t change yet.
LATER ON
On its payday, unrepresented, the pay less tax and dues goes into your account, and a notice says so.
Represented, it goes into your agency’s client trust account, shown in what you’re worth as held for you, and three business days later comes on to your account less commission.
Each payment counts on your tax return as wages, with what was withheld, the commission and the dues.
READING THE RESULT
Promised means it’s owed and not yet paid. In Trust means the production has paid and your agency holds it. Received gives the figures as they were posted.
Deferred pay is shown and never paid.
On an F-1 while you’re a student, outside acting work isn’t open to you: Yale grants CPT only for training the whole program requires, and Acting has none. On OPT after your degree, each day of an engagement counts as a day worked.
How It Connects
COMES FIRST
Permission To Work: Each engagement’s dates are read against your permission to work when you book it.
CHANGES LATER
Your Accounts: Each payment lands in your account on its payday, or from your agency’s trust account a few business days later.
COUNTS TOWARD
Your Tax Return: Your tax return reads each payment’s wages, withholding, commission, dues and the town the work was done in.
WHAT LEADS HERE
Commission: Commission comes off your pay as it passes through your agency’s trust account.
What To Do Next
Read Commission, to see what your agency takes and when.
If You Can’t
“No work booked. Work you accept appears here with its dates, its calls, its pay and any commission.”
MISTAKES, AND PUTTING THEM RIGHT
Counting promised pay as money you have. It isn’t in your accounts until its payday, or until your agency passes it on.
Thinking signing with an agent lets you work. Permission to work is your papers’, and an agreement changes nothing of it.
What Decides It
Representation
Whether you had an agreement in force on the day you made the contract, not on the payday
Where The Work Is
The town it’s done in, which your tax return reads: a New Haven resident’s New York work is taxed by New York, with Connecticut’s credit for it
Permission To Work
Read for each of its dates when you book it, and kept: the same permission Papers shows under Permission To Work
A Worked Example

An unrepresented graduate living in New Haven, booked for three weeks Off-Broadway at $1,051 a week from Monday, September 14, 2026.

Open Career, then Work.
Read the three payments, each promised and due a Thursday.
Move the clock past Thursday, September 24.

What comes of it. The first week is paid that Thursday at noon: tax and Equity’s working dues withheld, the rest into checking, and the year’s wages count it as New York work.
