His Office’s Answers And The Meeting
Show Less

His office answers an approach in one of four ways: it asks for more, asks to meet, passes, or doesn’t answer. Each answer comes as a notice, and History With His Office keeps every step.

A meeting is on your calendar at 11 AM, at the agency’s New York office or by video, and the clock stops at it like any commitment. You can move it once, a week on.

Where To Find It
Career, Representation, The Meeting.
When You Can Use It
From the guide’s chapter “Representation”.
Show Me Where
What To Press
When his office asks for more, make what it asked for on the Portfolio tab, then press Send What His Office Asked For.
When it asks to meet, be there: press Go when the clock stops at it.
If you can’t make it, press Ask To Move on the Representation tab, or at the stop.
What You Need First
For a request, what it asked for made by its date.
For a move, a meeting not moved before, and not yet started.
TIME AND MONEY
Time
The meeting’s hour, and the trip: two hours from New Haven (estimated)
Money
None
What Happens
STRAIGHT AWAY
What you send goes to his office, and its answer comes about a week later.
A moved meeting is on your calendar at its new time.
LATER ON
After a meeting you keep, his office tells you its decision five days later: an offer or a pass.
A meeting you miss closes the approach, since a meeting comes before an offer in the game.
READING THE RESULT
Material sent when asked, and meetings kept, count for you under Reliability; a missed one counts against you.
How It Connects
CHANGES LATER
The Clock: Play And Pause: A meeting stops the clock like any commitment, and offers Ask To Move at the stop.
COUNTS TOWARD
Why It Went This Way: A meeting kept or missed, and material sent when asked, are read under Reliability.
WHAT LEADS HERE
The Agent And How To Reach Him: Sending your materials puts his office’s answer on the way, about two weeks on.
What To Do Next
After a meeting, wait for the decision, then read the agreement if one comes.
If You Can’t
His office hasn’t asked you for anything.
You haven’t cut a reel yet.
In the game his office moves a meeting once.
MISTAKES, AND PUTTING THEM RIGHT
Sleeping through the meeting. A commitment slept through is missed.
What Decides It
A Request
What’s missing: a reel, newer headshots or a résumé brought up to date, due in three weeks (estimated)
A Move
Once, a week on (estimated)
A Worked Example

His office asked for a reel, and you have a self-tape to cut one from.

Cut a reel on the Portfolio tab.
Press Send What His Office Asked For.

What comes of it. The reel goes to his office, and its answer comes about a week later, as a notice.
