Audition towns: New York or Hudson
Two days later the casting office answers with an audition: in person in New York or Hudson at a set time, with the trip there, or a self-tape due by an evening. An audition in person goes on your calendar.
An audition is in person, in New York or Hudson at a set time, or a self-tape due by an evening. Prepare the sides first with Break It Down, Learn The Lines and Rehearse It On Your Feet, the way you prepare a practice brief. Then press Tape It, or be there when the clock stops for the audition and press Go.

Casting decides from how the audition went, how well the role suits you and how many other actors it saw. Some roles call you back for a second audition first.
