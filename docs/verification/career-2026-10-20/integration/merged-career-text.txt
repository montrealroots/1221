== prerequisite
career.overview -> documents.work: Permission To Work on the Overview is the same permission Papers shows under Permission To Work.
career.reel -> craft.brief-tape: A self-tape of a practice brief is the first recorded work a reel can be cut from.
career.agent -> career.headshots: A check refuses an approach with no headshots, since every submission starts with one.
career.agent -> career.resume: A check refuses an approach with no résumé, and a résumé behind your record is one thing his office can ask for.
career.pay -> documents.work: Each engagement’s dates are read against your permission to work when you book it.
career.opportunities -> career.headshots: A submission starts with your headshots: without them it can’t be sent.
career.offers -> documents.work: An offer can be accepted only where your permission to work covers every one of its days.
career.outside-work -> career.offers: An offer in term is accepted on the School’s approval, or against it with the warning said first.

== immediate
career.profile -> career.resume: Your résumé uses your professional name, and one made under another name reads as behind.
career.headshots -> career.overview: Once you have headshots, What’s Missing stops asking for them, until they’re more than two years old.
career.headshots -> bank.accounts: The session’s price comes out of your bank as a work cost.
career.resume -> career.overview: Once you have a résumé, What’s Missing stops asking for one, until your record moves on past it.
career.reel -> career.overview: Once you have a reel, What’s Missing stops asking for one, and says when newer work came after it.
career.commission -> career.pay: Commission comes off your pay as it passes through your agency’s trust account.
career.outside-work -> school.standing: Work in term without approval moves your standing to academic warning, and a second time to dismissal.

== history
career.profile -> craft.profile: Your special skills are your techniques on the Player Profile, at the level it shows.
career.resume -> productions.nights: A School production’s part goes on your résumé once its run has closed.
career.reasons -> career.agent: Each answer his office gives keeps its reasons here.
career.agreement -> career.agent: Accepting, declining and the agreement’s end each go in History With His Office.
career.unions -> career.resume: Your résumé lists your unions once you’re a member.
career.offers -> career.resume: The part goes on your professional credits, and your résumé reads as behind until it has it.

== scheduled
career.agent -> career.agent-answers: Sending your materials puts his office’s answer on the way, about two weeks on.
career.agent-answers -> day.clock: A meeting stops the clock like any commitment, and offers Ask To Move at the stop.
career.pay -> bank.accounts: Each payment lands in your account on its payday, or from your agency’s trust account a few business days later.
career.unions -> bank.accounts: Each union bill comes out of your bank on its day.
career.opportunities -> career.auditions: A submission puts the casting office’s answer on the clock, and the answer sets an audition.
career.auditions -> day.calendar: An audition in person goes on your calendar with its trip, and stops the clock when it comes.
career.offers -> day.calendar: Accepting puts each work day on your calendar, and each stops the clock for you.

== evaluation
career.agent-answers -> career.reasons: A meeting kept or missed, and material sent when asked, are read under Reliability.
career.reasons -> career.reel: Your reel’s best band is part of Your Work, and a reel is part of Stage And Screen.
career.pay -> taxes.return: Your tax return reads each payment’s wages, withholding, commission, dues and the town the work was done in.
career.commission -> taxes.return: The year’s commission counts toward the performing artist’s deduction the return works out.
career.unions -> taxes.return: Dues count toward the cost of your work on the year’s return.
career.auditions -> career.offers: Casting reads your audition’s band, with the role’s fit and the field, when it decides whether to offer you the role.
