Barclays cancels the card and posts a replacement by second-class Royal Mail within seven working days, or five if you order it in Online Banking. In an emergency, some branches give you a temporary debit card on the spot for £12. It lasts 28 days, and you can have two a year. A Barclaycard credit card is blocked for good from the app and replaced within seven working days. Barclays says it tries to pass the new debit card’s details to shops that take regular payments from the old one (Visa tells most big retailers); you have to tell any others yourself.

Barclays texts you about a suspicious payment and asks you to reply Y or N, or calls you with an automated call. It says it may delay or decline an unusual payment, or block the account, until it’s sure you made it.

If a card payment wasn’t you, press Report It As Not Mine within 396 days of the statement that showed it, and you pay at most £35 of it.
