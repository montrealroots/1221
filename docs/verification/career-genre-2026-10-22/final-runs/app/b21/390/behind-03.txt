Once you report a card lost or stolen, Chase declines anything new on it and sends a replacement with a new number. For a credit card, the account itself stays open and keeps its age on your credit record.

Chase watches cards around the clock and may text, send an app notification, email or call to check that unusual activity was you. Fraud alerts are automatic: you don’t need to sign up. Chase can decline a payment it believes may be fraud, but it doesn’t say it holds a payment while it waits for your reply.

If a debit card payment wasn’t you, press Report It As Not Mine within 2 business days of finding out, and you pay at most $50 of it. Within 60 days of the statement that showed it, you pay at most $500, and after that, you pay all of it. On a credit card, you pay at most $50 of one you report within 60 days of the statement that showed it.
