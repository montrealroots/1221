How This Works

Chase pays what the account can’t cover without an arrangement, and charges $34.00 for each item that overdraws it, at most 3 a day. There’s no fee on an item of $5.00 or less, or while the account is overdrawn by $50 or less. A fee waits until the end of the next business day, and if the account is back to −$50.00 or better by then, there’s none. Payments at the same moment post largest first. It covers up to about $500.

You haven’t opted in, so an everyday debit card purchase the account can’t cover goes on the card, or is declined, and costs no fee. Other payments, like autopay and standing orders, go into the overdraft either way. The law requires a US bank to ask you first.
