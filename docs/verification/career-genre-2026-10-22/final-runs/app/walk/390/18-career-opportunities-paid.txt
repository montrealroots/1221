The Project
Silver Assembly, a fictional animated television series, by Thimble Lane Entertainment, cast by Iwata Casting. SAG-AFTRA Television Animation. Open Its Page
The Part
Robin: A pirate cook who is afraid of heights.
How The Work Is Done
An Animated Series. An animated series, an episode’s lines recorded in a session every other week.
What It Asks
Animated and Comedy, about evenly. Written for an actor or an actress. Playing ages 28 to 38. The part asks for Vocal Expression, Voice-Over and Comedy, where 90 is mastered. Your Vocal Expression is at 67, your Voice-Over at 60 and your Comedy at 64.
When And Where
Three recording sessions from August 20, 2029 to September 17, 2029, at a studio in Queens in New York. Getting there takes about two hours each way from where you live.
Pay
It pays $1,402.00 a day for three days, an estimate.
The Audition
A self-tape, then a callback for those casting wants to see again.

Submissions close Monday, July 23, 2029 at 9:00 AM.
