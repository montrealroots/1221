Ivy, Paper Moons
The Project
Paper Moons, a fictional student film by a graduate film program’s thesis production, cast by the film’s director. SAG-AFTRA Student Film.
The Part
Ivy: The sister who stayed home, who learns first that her brother isn’t going back and has the summer to decide whether to say so.
What It Asks
Coming Of Age. Written for an actress. Playing ages 21 to 30. The part asks for On Camera and Scene Study, where 90 is mastered. Your On Camera is at 53 and your Scene Study at 71.
When And Where
Three shoot days from December 3, 2029 to December 5, 2029, at an apartment in Brooklyn in New York. Getting there takes about two hours each way from where you live.
Pay
The pay is deferred: a student film pays its actors only if it earns money later, and no film in the game does, so nothing is paid for its three days of work.
The Audition
In person.

The offer stands until Sunday, November 11, 2029 at 5:00 PM.
