Your Résumé
Show Less

Your résumé is made from your record: your name, your unions, your training, your professional credits, any Emmy nomination or win and Sundance selection, your School credits and your special skills. Making it takes an hour at your own desk, estimated, and costs nothing.

Once something new is on your record, it reads as behind, and the panel names what it’s missing.

Where To Find It
Career, Portfolio, Résumé.
When You Can Use It
From the guide’s chapter “Your Career”.
Show Me Where
What To Press
Open Career, then the Portfolio tab.
Under Résumé, press Make Your Résumé, or Update Your Résumé once you have one.
Open What It Says to read it line by line.
What You Need First
Free for an hour.
Something on your record it doesn’t have yet, for an update.
TIME AND MONEY
Time
an hour (estimated)
Money
None
What Happens
STRAIGHT AWAY
The clock moves on an hour, and the new résumé is on the panel with what it says.
LATER ON
A part played, a skill grown, a new name, the degree, a union, an Emmy nomination once it’s announced, a win once it’s presented, or a Sundance selection once the festival says so makes it behind, and What’s Missing names what’s new.
READING THE RESULT
Up To Date means it has everything on your record. Behind names what it’s missing.
How It Connects
CHANGES STRAIGHT AWAY
The Career Screen: Once you have a résumé, What’s Missing stops asking for one, until your record moves on past it.
KEPT IN YOUR RECORD
The Nights: A School production’s part goes on your résumé once its run has closed.
WHAT LEADS HERE
Your Professional Profile: Your résumé uses your professional name, and one made under another name reads as behind.
Agents And How To Reach One: A check refuses an approach with no résumé, and a résumé behind your record is one thing an office can ask for.
Your Unions: Your résumé lists your unions once you’re a member.
Offers And Booked Work: The part goes on your professional credits, and your résumé reads as behind until it has it.
What To Do Next
Cut a reel if you have recorded work, or book headshots if you haven’t.
If You Can’t
Your résumé already has everything on your record.
You’re busy then.
MISTAKES, AND PUTTING THEM RIGHT
Updating it before a run has closed. A School part goes on it once the run’s last night is played.
What Decides It
Training
As your record has it: the degree and its year, a candidate’s year, or the terms studied without the degree
Credits
Professional credits and School credits, apart
Unions
Equity from the degree, as the School’s Bulletin says, and SAG-AFTRA when your record has it
A Worked Example

A second-year student who played a part in a School production last term, with a résumé made before it.

Open Career, then Portfolio.
Read Résumé’s Standing, then press Update Your Résumé.

What comes of it. Before, it says it’s behind and names the part. After an hour, it’s up to date and lists the part under School credits.
