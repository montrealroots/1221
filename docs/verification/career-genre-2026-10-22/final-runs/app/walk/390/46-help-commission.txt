Commission
Show Less

Your agency takes commission only on covered work, stage, film and television in the US, that you contracted while your agreement was in force. It’s taken when your pay passes through its client trust account, and shown on its own line on the Work tab and in the deposit’s line in Money.

The rate is the unions’ and the law’s: 10% at most, 5% on an Equity contract paying $525.00 a week or less, and on SAG-AFTRA work only what you’re paid over scale.

Where To Find It
Career, Work, each payment’s Commission.
When You Can Use It
From the guide’s chapter “Your Career”. Commission is owed only on work you contracted while represented.
Show Me Where
What To Press
Open Career, then the Work tab.
Under each payment, read Commission: for one to come, what’s expected and why; for one received, what was taken.
If your agreement has ended, read Still Owed After An Agreement Ended for the payments that still carry it.
What You Need First
An agreement in force when you made the contract, or an offer your agency passed on in its term that you accepted within 60 days after it ended.
TIME AND MONEY
Covered Work
10% of the gross, the most New York lets an agency take for an engagement
Equity
5% on a weekly salary of $525.00 or less, 10% above it
SAG-AFTRA
Only what’s over scale, up to 10%: at scale plus ten percent you keep scale and your agency takes the ten
What Happens
STRAIGHT AWAY
Accepting work shows the commission it will carry, as expected, under each payment.
LATER ON
When the pay passes through the trust account, the commission comes off it and your account gets the rest, three business days after the payday.
After your agreement ends, a contract you made while it ran still carries commission, whoever represents you then.
READING THE RESULT
No commission: you weren’t represented when you made the contract, or the work isn’t covered, or it pays SAG-AFTRA scale and nothing over it.
Commission is part of what your tax return counts as the cost of your work.
How It Connects
CHANGES STRAIGHT AWAY
Your Work And Its Pay: Commission comes off your pay as it passes through your agency’s trust account.
COUNTS TOWARD
Your Tax Return: The year’s commission counts toward the performing artist’s deduction the return works out.
What To Do Next
Read Your Unions, for the dues taken from union pay.
If You Can’t
“No commission: you weren’t represented when you made this contract.”
MISTAKES, AND PUTTING THEM RIGHT
Expecting an agreement signed after you accepted some work to take commission on it. Only contracts made while it’s in force carry it.
What Decides It
The Contract’s Date
The day you accepted the work, against your agreement’s dates
The Union
Equity’s tiers by the weekly salary, SAG-AFTRA’s scale by the rate
A Worked Example

Represented, you book two days on a network drama at scale plus ten, $1,411.30 a day.

Read the payment’s Commission on the Work tab.
Move the clock past its payday and three business days.

What comes of it. Commission is $256.60, what the $2,822.60 is over scale, so you keep the $2,566.00 scale before tax.
