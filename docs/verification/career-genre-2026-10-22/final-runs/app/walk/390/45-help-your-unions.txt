Your Unions
Show Less

Your degree makes you a member of Actors’ Equity, as the School’s Bulletin says, or your first day under an Equity contract does, if that comes first. Its initiation fee is $1,800.00, paid within three years with $600.00 down, and basic dues are $176.00 a year, billed each May and November.

A SAG-AFTRA principal role on a TV show or a theatrical or Low Budget film makes you eligible to join SAG-AFTRA. From the 30th day after that job began, SAG-AFTRA work needs you to be a member: press Join SAG-AFTRA, about $3,244.07.

Where To Find It
Career, Work, Your Unions.
When You Can Use It
From the guide’s chapter “Your Career”. Join SAG-AFTRA opens once you’ve worked a SAG-AFTRA principal role.
Show Me Where
What To Press
Open Career, then the Work tab.
Under Your Unions, read Equity’s bills: each paid, owed or due, with its date.
Once SAG-AFTRA says you can join, press Join SAG-AFTRA before you take SAG-AFTRA work past its 30 days.
What You Need First
For Equity: the degree, or a first day of work under an Equity contract.
For SAG-AFTRA: a SAG-AFTRA principal role worked, and the fee in your bank.
TIME AND MONEY
Equity’s Initiation Fee
$1,800.00 within three years: $600.00 when you join, then two parts a year apart. The two parts’ dates are estimated
Equity’s Dues
$88.00 each May and November, and 2.5% of Equity pay, taken from it. Taking it from the pay is estimated
Joining SAG-AFTRA
About $3,244.07: the initiation fee and half a year’s base dues, then half a year’s dues each May and November, and 1.575% of SAG-AFTRA pay. Read from a search engine’s copy of its pages (estimated)
What Happens
STRAIGHT AWAY
Join SAG-AFTRA takes its fee from your bank and makes you a member today. Your next SAG-AFTRA work takes its working dues.
LATER ON
Each union bill is taken from your bank on its day. If the bank can’t cover it, it’s owed under Bills Owed and paid off the first day there’s money.
Union pay has its working dues taken before it reaches you.
READING THE RESULT
A bill marked owed is a debt: it’s paid off when money comes in.
Ultra Low Budget films and student films don’t count toward SAG-AFTRA eligibility in the game.
How It Connects
CHANGES LATER
Your Accounts: Each union bill comes out of your bank on its day.
COUNTS TOWARD
Your Tax Return: Dues count toward the cost of your work on the year’s return.
KEPT IN YOUR RECORD
Your Résumé: Your résumé lists your unions once you’re a member.
WHAT LEADS HERE
Contracts, Residuals And A Series’ Seasons: A further episode or an option taken up past SAG-AFTRA’s thirty days needs you to be a member.
What To Do Next
Check Money for the bills on their days.
If You Can’t
“You can join SAG-AFTRA once you’ve worked a SAG-AFTRA principal role: a TV, theatrical or Low Budget film part. Ultra Low Budget and student films don’t count toward it.”
“You’re already a member of SAG-AFTRA, since …”
Your bank can’t cover the fee.
MISTAKES, AND PUTTING THEM RIGHT
Booking SAG-AFTRA work past the 30 days without joining first. The work is refused until you join.
What Decides It
The Degree Or An Equity Contract
Equity’s membership date, the degree’s or a first day of work under an Equity contract’s, which its first bill reads
Your First SAG-AFTRA Role
Its first day, from which you can join and after 30 days must, by federal law’s limit for a union contract
A Worked Example

A graduate whose degree was conferred on September 3, 2026.

Open Career, then Work.
Read Your Unions.

What comes of it. Equity’s $600.00 down was taken that day; $600.00 is due on September 3, 2027 and again on September 3, 2028; and $88.00 basic dues come on November 1, 2026.
