The Agreement
Show Less

When an agent offers to represent you, The Agreement shows every term before you answer, each marked A Published Rule or The Game’s Own Term. It runs a year from the day you sign it, and the offer stands two weeks.

Signing changes nothing at the School and gives no permission to work. It can end by agreement at any time, or under Equity’s or SAG-AFTRA’s rules, or when its year is up. You have one agent at a time: another agent’s agreement can’t start while one runs.

Where To Find It
Career, Representation, The Agreement.
When You Can Use It
From the guide’s chapter “Representation”.
Show Me Where
What To Press
Read every term under The Agreement.
Press Accept The Agreement, or Decline The Agreement.
While it runs, press End It By Agreement, End It: No Offer In 90 Days or End It: Under Ten Days’ Work. A grayed one says when it can.
What You Need First
For Accept or Decline, an offer still standing.
For End It: No Offer In 90 Days, 90 days with no real offer of covered work through the agent, the days you’re employed on covered work not counted, and not while you’re working.
For End It: Under Ten Days’ Work, 151 days from signing, and fewer than ten days of covered work in the last 91.
TIME AND MONEY
Time
None
Money
Commission on covered work: 10% of gross pay, or 5% on an Equity contract paying $525.00 a week or less
What Happens
STRAIGHT AWAY
Accepting makes the agent yours from today. Declining closes the approach.
LATER ON
An offer you don’t answer lapses after its two weeks.
When it ends, commission stays owed on contracts you made while it ran, and The Agreement says which.
READING THE RESULT
A Published Rule follows the unions’ or New York’s published rules. The Game’s Own Term is the game’s: an agent’s own agreement isn’t published.
How It Connects
KEPT IN YOUR RECORD
Agents And How To Reach One: Accepting, declining and the agreement’s end each go in the agent’s History panel.
WORTH DOING WITH IT
Permission To Work: Signing with an agent gives no permission to work: Papers, under Permission To Work, still says what you may do.
What To Do Next
Keep your availability and your materials current: covered work comes through the agent.
If You Can’t
He hasn’t offered you an agreement.
You’re not represented by him.
Equity’s rule lets you end it once 90 days go by with no real offer of covered work through him.
In the game you have one agent at a time, so this agreement can’t start while that one runs.
MISTAKES, AND PUTTING THEM RIGHT
Ending it while a contract made in the term is still to be paid, and expecting no commission. It stays owed.
What Decides It
How Long
One year from signing (estimated)
Ending It
Equity’s 90 days and SAG-AFTRA’s 151 and 91 days, from their published rules
