Work While You’re At The School
Show Less

The School’s Bulletin discourages outside work in term and approves it “In rare situations” for short, professionally meaningful work asked for in good time. Press Ask The School on a role whose days fall in term: the dean decides seven days later.

Work taken in term without approval brings an academic warning the first time and dismissal the second. Summer and the winter break are yours, unless the School holds you for one of its calls.

Where To Find It
Career, Opportunities, Open Calls.
When You Can Use It
From the guide’s chapter “Finding Work”, while you’re a student and a role’s days fall in term.
Show Me Where
What To Press
Open Career, then Opportunities, and find a role whose days fall in term.
Read what the School looks at, under the role, and whether each condition holds.
Press Ask The School, at least two weeks before the first day.
What You Need First
Good standing at the School.
A role whose days fall in term, not settled yet.
Permission to work outside the School: on an F-1, there’s none while you’re a student.
TIME AND MONEY
Time
An hour to talk to the chair and write the proposal (estimated)
Money
None
What Happens
STRAIGHT AWAY
The request is with the chair and the dean, and the role says when the dean decides.
LATER ON
A week on, the dean approves or refuses it, and the notice says each condition. Approved, the class days it takes are excused.
READING THE RESULT
The School’s rule is the Bulletin’s. The fourteen days, ten working days and two class days, and the dean’s week, are the game’s estimates of its words.
How It Connects
COMES FIRST
Offers And Booked Work: An offer in term is accepted on the School’s approval, or against it with the warning said first.
CHANGES STRAIGHT AWAY
Your Standing And Classes: Work in term without approval moves your standing to academic warning, and a second time to dismissal.
What To Do Next
Submit for the role, and accept the offer once the dean has approved it.
If You Can’t
Only a student in good standing may ask for outside work.
None of its days fall in term, so the School’s approval isn’t needed.
Your F-1 doesn’t let you work for an employer outside the School while you’re a student.
MISTAKES, AND PUTTING THEM RIGHT
Accepting work in term before the dean decides. If the dean refuses, working it brings an academic warning.
What Decides It
In Good Time
Asked 14 days or more before the first day (estimated)
Short
10 working days or fewer (estimated)
Class Time
No School or Yale Rep call, and two class days at most (estimated)
A Worked Example

A first-year in good standing, and a two-day shoot in October posted five weeks ahead.

Press Ask The School on the role.
A week later, read the dean’s answer.

What comes of it. The chair recommends it and the dean approves it: two working days, asked well ahead, a paid professional role, no School call and at most two class days. Those class days are excused.
