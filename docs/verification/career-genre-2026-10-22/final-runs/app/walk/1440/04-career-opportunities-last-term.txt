Marlowe Ashdown, The Accounts On Lyle Street

It’s a new casting of the part. The last time it was posted to you, on October 2, 2028: Submissions for Marlowe Ashdown closed on October 9, 2028.

The Project
The Accounts On Lyle Street, a fictional animated television series, by Sable Lane Television, cast by Delacroix Casting. SAG-AFTRA Television Animation. Open Its Page
The Part
Marlowe Ashdown: A detective owl who is always one step behind.
How The Work Is Done
An Animated Series. An animated series, an episode’s lines recorded in a session every other week.
What It Asks
Animated and Comedy, about evenly. Written for an actor or an actress. Playing ages 24 to 36. The part asks for Vocal Expression, Voice-Over and Comedy, where 90 is mastered. Your Vocal Expression is at 66, your Voice-Over at 60 and your Comedy at 64.
When And Where
Three recording sessions from February 12, 2029 to March 12, 2029, at a studio in Santa Clarita in Los Angeles. It’s a trip the production books: about 11 hours door to door each way, by car and by air, landing at least 36 hours before the first call.
Pay
It pays $1,361.00 a day for three days, an estimate.
The Audition
A self-tape, then a callback for those casting wants to see again.

Submissions close Monday, January 15, 2029 at 9:00 AM.
