Why It Went This Way
Show Less

Why It Went This Way gives the game’s reasons for the office’s last decision, and where they stand now. For Thomas Ashgrove there are seven, each set apart: your work, stage and screen, training and experience, fit with his department, availability, room on his list, and reliability. A real agent’s list isn’t known, so for one there are six, without room on the list. Each is short of a bar the game sets, near it, at it or beyond it.

An approach goes further only with four of them at the bar or beyond, and then against the year’s other actors. Better work raises the chance, and nothing makes it certain.

Where To Find It
Career, Representation, Why It Went This Way.
When You Can Use It
From the guide’s chapter “Representation”.
Show Me Where
What To Press
Open Career, then the Representation tab.
Under Why It Went This Way, press Show The Reasons.
What You Need First
The notice read.
TIME AND MONEY
Time
None: reading it moves no clock
Money
None
What Happens
STRAIGHT AWAY
The table opens, and closes with Hide The Reasons.
LATER ON
As Things Stand Now changes with your record: a new credit, a better reel, the degree, a meeting kept.
READING THE RESULT
None of it is the agent’s own. The bar and the chances are the game’s, and Room On His List is assumed. For a real agent, how likely the office is to take you further is one assumption for every real agent in the game.
At The Last Decision is kept as it was, so it reads the same after your record moves on.
How It Connects
COUNTS TOWARD
Your Reel: Your reel’s best band is part of Your Work, and a reel is part of Stage And Screen.
KEPT IN YOUR RECORD
Agents And How To Reach One: Each answer an office gives keeps its reasons here.
WHAT LEADS HERE
An Office’s Answers And The Meeting: A meeting kept or missed, and material sent when asked, are read under Reliability.
What To Do Next
Work on what’s short: a reel, newer work, a credit.
If You Can’t
The reasons are there once you’ve read the notice about the agent.
MISTAKES, AND PUTTING THEM RIGHT
Reading one factor as the decision. It takes enough of them at the bar, and then the competition.
What Decides It
Your Work
The best judged band of the work the office has: your showcase scenes once the School follows them up, your reel, a referring job
Room On His List
For Thomas Ashgrove, one to three openings a year, by billing and age, drawn for each year (estimated)
The Bar
four of the factors at it or beyond (estimated)
A Worked Example

A graduate with one job judged Strong and no reel.

Press Show The Reasons.

What comes of it. Your Work is At The Bar and Stage And Screen Near The Bar, until a reel shows screen work too.
