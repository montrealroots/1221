Missing a performance or an understudy call is a grave breach: it puts you straight on academic warning, or dismisses you if you’re already on warning.

At the term review, a term falls short if your attendance or your growth is under its minimum, if a run on stage went mostly off, if you fail a course, or if half or more of your coursework due was Unready or not shown (once 3 or more pieces are due). A term that falls short moves your standing one step: from good standing to academic concern, then to academic warning, then to dismissal. A term that meets them all puts you back in good standing.

After your last term, Commencement confers the degree if you’re in good standing or have a concern; if your last term ends on academic warning, the faculty don’t recommend it.
