Missing a performance or an understudy call is a grave breach: it puts you straight on academic warning, or dismisses you if you’re already on warning.

At the term review, a term falls short if your attendance or your growth is under its minimum, if a run on stage went mostly off, if you fail a course, or if half or more of your coursework due was Unready or not shown (once 3 or more pieces are due). What nerves do to a performance

COMPOSURE
53

Composure keeps your work consistent under pressure, in a scene, a song, a self-tape, an audition and a night on stage, and steadies you after a slip. It grows a little with every night in front of an audience and every solo showing. To train it, practice Graded Exposure under Attributes And Craft on your Player Profile.
