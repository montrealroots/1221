Your Composure

What nerves do to a performance

COMPOSURE
53
Nerves add 2.8 stress before a performance

Composure keeps your work consistent under pressure, in a scene, a song, a self-tape, an audition and a night on stage, and steadies you after a slip. It grows a little with every night in front of an audience and every solo showing. To train it, practice Graded Exposure under Attributes And Craft on your Player Profile.
