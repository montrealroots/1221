The Part
Jess: One of two night-shift radio hosts who start hearing their own voices call in.
What It Asks
Psychological Horror. Written for an actor or an actress. Playing ages 21 to 32. The part asks for On Camera and Emotional Depth, where 90 is mastered. Your On Camera is at 48 and your Emotional Depth at 54.

Four shoot days from October 5, 2026 to October 8, 2026, at a motel upstate in New York. Getting there takes about two hours each way from where you live.

The Project
Paper Moons, a fictional student film by a graduate film program’s thesis production, cast by the film’s director. SAG-AFTRA Student Film.
The Part
Ivy: The sister who stayed home, who learns first that her brother isn’t going back and has the summer to decide whether to say so.
What It Asks
Coming Of Age. Written for an actress. Playing ages 21 to 30. The part asks for On Camera and Scene Study, where 90 is mastered. Your On Camera is at 48 and your Scene Study at 55.
When And Where
Three shoot days from October 5, 2026 to October 7, 2026, at an apartment in Brooklyn in New York. Getting there takes about two hours each way from where you live.
Pay
The pay is deferred: a student film pays its actors only if it earns money later, and no film in the game does, so nothing is paid for its three days of work.
The Audition
In person.

Submissions close Monday, September 7, 2026 at 9:00 AM.
