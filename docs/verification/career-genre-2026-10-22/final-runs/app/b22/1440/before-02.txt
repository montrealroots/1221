How This Works

On an F-1 you may work only at the School: up to 19 hours a week in term (Yale’s limit) and full time in the breaks, and only while your program lasts. That work lets you get a Social Security number. You apply in person at the office on Court Street, with your passport, your I-20 and letters from the international office and the School, and the card comes in two to three weeks. Without a number, your 1040-NR goes in with an ITIN application, which holds up its refund by seven weeks, and a bank will open an account for you only in a branch.

OPT

Form I-765, (c)(3)(B), after your degree

How This Works

Your window to apply was March 2, 2029 to July 30, 2029, 90 days before your program ends to 60 days after. OPT is permission to work in your field after your degree, and it keeps your US status going. Every day from the first day of a job to its last counts as a day with work, and every other day as one without, the days before a job you’ve booked begins among them. An approved OPT ends once you’ve had more than 90 days without work in total, and your US status ends with it.

So far your OPT has counted 88 days without work and 3 days with work.

It costs $470.00. Premium processing costs $1,780.00 more and gets you a decision within 30 business days. OPT follows a completed degree: if school ends without yours, it’s denied when it’s decided, and the fee isn’t refunded.
