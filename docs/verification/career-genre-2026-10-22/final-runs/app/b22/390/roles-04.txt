Delphine Strand, The Telegram Of Fenwick Pass
The Project
The Telegram Of Fenwick Pass, a fictional ultra-low-budget feature film, by Lowmoor Yard Pictures, cast by Mbeki Casting. SAG-AFTRA Ultra Low Budget. Open Its Page
The Part
Delphine Strand: A pastry chef who swore never to fall for a client.
How The Work Is Done
An Independent Feature. A live-action feature on an independent budget: two takes a scene and time for only one more in a day, the scenes shot by location, and a release in cinemas a year on.
What It Asks
Drama and Romance, about evenly. Written for an actress. Playing ages 20 to 29. The part asks for On Camera and Scene Study, where 90 is mastered. Your On Camera is at 48 and your Scene Study at 55.
When And Where
Four shoot days from October 5, 2026 to October 8, 2026, at a stage in Burbank and locations in the Valley in Los Angeles. It’s a trip the production books: about 11 hours door to door each way, by car and by air, landing at least 36 hours before the first call.
Pay
It pays $257.00 a day for four days, the SAG-AFTRA Ultra Low Budget Day Performer minimum.
The Audition
A self-tape.

Submissions close Monday, August 31, 2026 at 9:00 AM.

Reese Ashdown, The Margins
The Project
The Margins, a fictional animated television series, by Rook Hollow Studios, cast by Iwata Casting. SAG-AFTRA Television Animation. Open Its Page
The Part
Reese Ashdown: A robot gardener who has a secret map.
How The Work Is Done
An Animated Series. An animated series, an episode’s lines recorded in a session every other week.
What It Asks
Animated and Children’s, about evenly. Written for an actor or an actress. Playing ages 19 to 31. The part asks for Vocal Expression and Voice-Over, where 90 is mastered. Your Vocal Expression is at 61 and your Voice-Over at 56.
When And Where
Two recording sessions from September 28, 2026 to October 19, 2026, at a stage in Hollywood in Los Angeles. It’s a trip the production books: about 11 hours door to door each way, by car and by air, landing at least 36 hours before the first call.
Pay
It pays $1,283.00 a day for two days, an estimate.
The Audition
A self-tape.

Submissions close Monday, August 31, 2026 at 9:00 AM.
