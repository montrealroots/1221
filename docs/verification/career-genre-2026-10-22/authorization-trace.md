# Brief 22, A2: Permission To Work Through An Engagement's Life

Where the game reads permission to work in the United States at each stage of a professional engagement, before brief 22
(`69996adb`) and after it (`8aa87539`, the tested commit's `src`). Line numbers are the tested commit's. The rule each
read applies, and the sources it rests on, are in `authorization-sources.md`; the tests that hold each stage are named
beside it.

| Stage | Before brief 22 | After brief 22 | Held by |
|---|---|---|---|
| Submitting, and an audition in person, taped or a callback | Not read. | Not read, as before: an audition is a try for work, not work. | (unchanged) |
| Preparing the sides or the part | Not read. | Not read, as before: an actor's own study. | (unchanged) |
| The offer | Accept refused unless `usWorkAuthorized` held at each day's start (`acceptRefusal`). Nothing said of OPT's days without work. | Refused unless permission holds for each day whole, its start and its end (`workAuthorizedFor`, `systems/projobs.ts:865`); and where OPT's days without work would run out before the first day, Accept says so before it's pressed (`acceptWarning`, `systems/projobs.ts:891`), since a booking counts as no work until it begins. | `tests/b22-authorization.test.ts`: "before", "inside a day" |
| What permission said, kept | `eligibilityRead` at acceptance, each date's noon, on the engagement (`systems/propay.ts:223`), shown on Papers. | The same, and its words for OPT say a job counts as work from its first day to its last. | `tests/propay-screen.test.ts` |
| OPT's days counted | Weekly (`OPT_WEEK_KIND`): seven days less those touched by any engagement not called off, booked or worked. Ended at the week's end, up to six days after the 91st. | Daily, each New York day once it's over (`settleOpt`, `systems/permission.ts:312`), at every midnight (`documents.opt_day`, `systems/permission.ts:474`), a day with work only inside an engagement begun and not ended whose hours average 20 a week (`employmentSpan`, `:269`). An older save's count goes on from where its weeks reached (`countedFrom`, `:298`). | "A4 to A9", "A12", "a booking isn't employment…" |
| A rehearsal, a performance or a shoot day | Not read: the day happened, judged, recorded, practised, attended, enjoyed and credited, whatever permission said. | Read at the day's own hour: OPT's days counted up to it first (`settleOpt`), then the day read whole (`workAuthorizedFor`); without permission the day doesn't happen and the work is released (`systems/projobs.ts:1497-1501`). | "A1", "A7, during", "A12, an older save", "inside a day" |
| Permission ends between work days | Nothing: work booked past it went on. | OPT ended for its days without work emits `PERMISSION_ENDED` (`endOpt`, `systems/permission.ts:335`); the work system releases every engagement with a day still to come (`pro.release_unauthorized`, `systems/projobs.ts:1559`), its days off the calendar at once. An expiry on the end date is found at the next work day. | "A1", "before", "during" |
| Released | (No such thing.) | Called off from then (`callOff`, `systems/projobs.ts:1285`), "Released As {part}" said with why and what's kept (`release`, `:1337`), the history noted. | "A1", "A7, during" |
| Pay | Paid on each payday for an engagement not called off; one called off was owed its worked weeks by `callOff` but `payDue` skipped it (`systems/propay.ts`), so they were never paid. | A week with a day worked stays owed and is paid on its payday (`payDue`, `systems/propay.ts:473`); a week with nothing worked is dropped (`callOff`). | "A7, during", "authorized work and after", `tests/pro-work.test.ts` |
| Credits | A stage credit at the first night played, a screen credit at the finish. | The same, so a run released before a night has no credit, one released after keeps it, and a shoot released before its last day has none. | "A1", "A7, during", "inside a day" |
| The finish | When the last day was worked. | The same: a released engagement never finishes. | (unchanged) |
| Papers | Your US Status ended with the thirteenth weekly count. | Your US Status ends on the day OPT's days without work run out, the work booked read as if it goes ahead from its first day (`optRunsOut`, `systems/permission.ts:348`), said as the last day in status (`src/ui/papers/Documents.tsx`, found on the built app the same day a day late). | `tests/evidence2-b06.test.ts`, `tests/evidence-g16-papers.test.ts`, `app/walk.mjs` |
| Recovery | (Nothing ended.) | No route in play: OPT isn't granted twice and a green card is refused; a permission held from elsewhere (an older save's) lets new work be taken, and the released work stays released. | "A11" |
