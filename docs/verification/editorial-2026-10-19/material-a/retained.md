# Material-A: A Sample Of What Was Kept

Every kept source has its own reason in `docs/text/second-pass/material-a.json`. These are a sample, across the files,
with the words kept and the reason, so the owner can judge the reasons as well as the rewrites.

## Batch 1

- **`briefs-a.ts` [brief-absurdist], Proof Of Arrival.** Relationship: "Strangers across a counter. He has never
  refused anyone, and he has never registered anyone either." Obstacle: "Each rule the clerk explains needs the thing
  the last rule forbids, and he means every word kindly." *Reason:* it sets out the witness rule's loop in plain,
  playable facts (one window, closing at five, a clerk who means every word kindly), and each beat says what Margit
  does.
- **`briefs-a.ts` [whisper], Not A Sound.** "The silence is where the comedy comes from: she fights the whole scene at
  a whisper, and the strain of staying quiet is the joke." It rests on: "The speeches have started on the other side of
  a thin door." *Reason:* one plain, playable rule for the scene, resting on the speeches beyond a thin door.
- **`briefs-a.ts` [brief-art_house], Oranges.** Beats: "He talks; she peels, and the peel comes off in one piece." /
  "He stops and waits. She offers him a segment." / "Her one full sentence: she asks him to leave the table where it
  is." *Reason:* a scene of silence and one long take, built from exact, unshowy facts an actor can play. The segment
  is the orange's, so the engine-speak count's hit on it stays.
- **`plays.ts` #THESIS_PLAYS.** "Hedda Gabler", "Henrik Ibsen", "Hedda Tesman", "Judge Brack" and the rest. *Reason:*
  the plays' real titles, authors and characters; a title is also matched by exact text against the casting a save
  holds (`school.ts`, `p.title === c.play`), so it's a key.
