Your passport and ID are on Papers, under Passport And ID, and your student status and what follows it are under Permission To Work. Press any document you hold to see who issued it, the last digits of its number, when it was issued and when it runs out, and what it lets you do today. A passport comes from the country you’re a citizen of, at its passport office’s fee and in its office’s time. Under Your Passport, press Apply For One, or Renew It if you have one, and, where the office sells a faster service, Apply Faster or Renew It Faster. Applying only starts it: you hold the new passport once it’s issued, and it cancels the old one, along with any travel authorization issued on it. Your ID is the ID card of the state you live in. Under Your ID, press Get with the card’s name to apply for one at its fee. Connecticut mails it, and you can use it as photo ID once it arrives. A new bank asks to see valid photo ID (a passport, a license or an ID card) before it takes you on. A license that runs out can’t be renewed in the game, so use an ID card or a passport instead. Report Lost cancels a passport, a license, an international driving permit or an ID card, and Report Stolen does the same for one that was stolen. A lost passport cancels whatever was issued on it too, except an F-1. A lost license is replaced by the state that issued it if you’re living there, or by mail from out of state where the issuer sends one, as New York does. In New York a replacement is free if the license was stolen and the police have your report: use File A Police Report, online where the police take reports that way, as New Haven’s do. A lost ID card is replaced with Replace It by the state that issued it, if you live there, at its fee, and it arrives after the issuer’s published wait. Going Abroad shows what each country asks of someone with your citizenship: whether you can just go, need a travel authorization first, or need a visa. Pick a country to see what it asks for and what you’re missing. You can’t travel in the game, so this is information, not a form to fill in. A visa or a travel authorization covers a visit only: staying, or working, needs a different permission. On an F-1, you’re in US status while you study and for sixty days after your program ends. After the degree, Apply For OPT, on Permission To Work, asks for twelve months’ permission to work in your field, which you lose after 90 days in all without work. Residence Abroad says what another country asks before you can make a home there. Read “Money” next, about everything you have and owe. The Details Some countries want your passport to be valid for some months past the day you leave. Where an office’s fee from abroad couldn’t be found, as with New Zealand’s GST, the fine print says so and the fee is estimated. For anyone who isn’t a citizen, an ID card’s term is estimated too, since the state doesn’t publish one, and Your ID says so. Replacing a lost ID card costs the issuer’s fee: $8 in New York, $30 in Connecticut, $40 in California (free from 62), $10 in Ontario, $15 in British Columbia, nothing at the INE, and €35 for Ireland’s passport card.
