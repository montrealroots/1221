What Needs You, on Home, is where you find out what’s happened and what’s waiting for you: groceries you couldn’t pay for, a late card payment, a teacher’s note on your work, a birthday. The number beside Home in the menu counts the notices and questions waiting. Press a notice’s title to read it in full. If it has a Go There button, that takes you to the screen it’s about. Once you’ve seen them, press Mark Read, which shows how many are unread, to clear them from the panel. Some notices are questions, with their answers on buttons. Dismiss takes a question off the list without answering it, but what it was about still stands: a tax return is still due, and you can still deal with it on its own screen. When nothing is waiting, the panel tells you about anything that still needs your attention, such as money you owe or two commitments at the same time. Read “How You Are” next, about your energy, stress and readiness.
