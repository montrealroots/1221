Press your name on Home, or the line at the top of How You Are, to open your own page. It shows your whole wellbeing and your readiness, your traits, any long-term condition and any injury, and you can press each of them for more. Your birthday comes every year, at nine in the morning. The age you turn changes how far your body can go and how much a night’s sleep gives back. Every life ends. Each year carries the same chance of death as a real year at your age, from the US life table, adjusted for life expectancy where you live (known for fourteen countries), higher for a worn-down body and lower for one kept well. The game never tells you when. When it ends, the clock stops for good, and the save shows who you were, what you earned and what you left behind. It stays in its slot until you delete it.
