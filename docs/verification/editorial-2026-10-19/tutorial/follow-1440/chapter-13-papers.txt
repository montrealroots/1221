Papers holds your health cover, your benefits and pension, and your tax returns. Open it with the Papers button on the Papers line in Your Affairs on Home, or from Papers in the menu. The Papers line shows what covers you, any unemployment you’re paid, and anything that needs doing, such as a return that’s due. Its tabs are Tax Returns, Health Cover, Unemployment, Passport And ID, and Permission To Work. While you’re at school, the Yale Health plan covers you, billed each term, and it pays for a doctor’s visit only at Yale Health Center. Your Cover, on Health Cover, says what covers you and what it costs. To see a doctor, choose a practice under Your Doctor on Health Cover, then press Book An Appointment. In New Haven, Yale Health Center sees a student it covers the next weekday, at no charge. A visit you keep is seen and billed; one you miss isn’t, and you can book again. Each piece of care brings a bill for what your plan doesn’t pay, under Care And Its Bills. Left unpaid, it becomes a medical debt, and Pay It Off settles it. With insurance, Apply For Assistance puts a Connecticut hospital bill in for the hospital’s financial assistance before it’s due. Yale’s plan covers you to the end of July after school. From 60 days before it ends, Enroll, under The Marketplace on Health Cover, takes you into the cover you qualify for: Medicaid or a state program where your income is low enough, or a marketplace plan. Enrolling starts it, but you’re covered only from its start date, usually the first of the next month. Drop The Plan ends a plan of your own. A long-term condition you carry, such as asthma, costs its care each month, under Your Conditions: Let It Go stops the care, and Keep It Up starts it again. Depression can be lifted by a course of therapy: choose a Therapist where you live and press Begin A Course Of 8. Out of paid work, File A Claim, on Unemployment, opens unemployment insurance where you live, if your wages qualify. A state pension is claimed there too, once your record and your age allow. Each February, a year’s tax return is ready on Tax Returns if it owes or is owed anything or its income reached the standard deduction, and every year on an F-1. Press File It by mid-April. A balance is paid from your bank, savings included, and filing late with a balance costs penalties. Whatever the bank couldn’t pay grows until you settle it with Pay. A police report belongs to a paper you’ve reported lost or stolen: File A Police Report, beside it on Passport And ID, files one online where the police take reports that way. Reporting a paper lost opens with the next chapter. Next, “Passports And ID” covers your passport, your ID, and your permission to study and work. The Details Enroll takes you into Medicaid (Medi-Cal in California, HUSKY D in Connecticut), New York’s Essential Plan or Covered Connecticut whenever last month’s income is low enough, with no premium. Cover starts from the first of the next month, HUSKY D from the first of the month you enroll, or as Yale’s ends if that’s later. From 2027 Medicaid asks for a month spent partly at the School, or one with $580 of income (pay, interest or a benefit counts), unless you’re 65 or over, on SNAP under its work rules or, if you ask, in hospital that month: in Connecticut the month before you apply, and any one of the 6 before a renewal. Short of it, Apply For HUSKY D sends a notice giving 30 days from its receipt to answer, and a renewal’s notice keeps you covered meanwhile; Request A Hardship Exception answers it with a stay in hospital. Covered Connecticut asks an income over HUSKY D’s line and at least the poverty line, and from 2027 a citizen’s or green card’s status, and ends once Yale Health covers you again. A program is checked again at renewal, every six months from 2027. Where last month’s income now fits the other program, it moves you there with no gap. If you fit neither, or its work rules aren’t met, it ends at a month’s end, with 10 days’ notice or more, and it ends if you move out of the state. Otherwise Enroll buys the marketplace’s plan in New York, California or Connecticut, starting the same way: in open enrollment, in the 60 days before Yale Health ends, or within 60 days of losing cover. A plan you drop or leave unpaid opens no such window. Each month you live in California without cover adds California’s penalty to your return. A marketplace plan may deny a claim, as KFF finds one in five are: it’s billed as if you were uninsured, and Appeal It within 180 days has it decided in 60. New York and California don’t pay the first week of a claim that would pay; Connecticut does. A week with work pays less, or nothing, and a week abroad pays nothing. Living in Britain as a British or Irish citizen, File A Claim is Universal Credit: a month assessed at a time and paid a week after, the standard allowance, less 55p of each pound earned and something for savings over £6,000, none over £16,000. In Ireland it’s Jobseeker’s Allowance, €254 a week for the days out of work, less something for savings over €20,000. A state pension is claimed under Unemployment: Claim Social Security, or Claim It for the UK’s or Ireland’s State Pension, the IMSS pension or the Pensión para el Bienestar. A pension has federal tax withheld from each month. Illness comes at a young adult’s rates, and the flu more often when you’re short of sleep or stressed, in the weeks your town’s climate brings it. A Connecticut hospital’s financial assistance comes off an uninsured patient’s bill at once; with insurance, Apply For Assistance is free to 250% of the poverty line and 67% off to 550%. Buy Dental adds a dental plan of your own where your health plan pays nothing for the dentist, in New York or beside a Covered California plan; Drop Dental ends it, though Yale’s, paid for the year, runs to its end. On a 1040-NR with no wages, the return is due by June 15, though Connecticut’s return on it is still due in mid-April. A UK tax year’s is settled on its own, by the next January 31. The IRS’s refund comes 21 days after filing, 7 weeks later on a 1040-NR filed without a Social Security number, and a state’s on the state’s own day. What the bank couldn’t pay grows from the due date by the IRS’s 7% a year in interest and a penalty of 0.5% a month, to 25% of it. The IRS bills it at filing; left alone, from the due date it writes every five weeks, its last letter a final notice, and 30 days on it takes the balance from your bank accounts. A return never filed that owes, the IRS files for you 365 days after it was due, from what your employers and banks reported. Your own, filed within 120 days, replaces it before any of it is assessed; filed after, it makes the tax what your return says, and the penalty and interest fall in proportion. A doctor’s visit is an appointment where the place publishes how long one is waited for: England’s spread from the same day to five weeks, New York’s four days and Los Angeles’s thirteen, Canada’s next day for about a quarter, IMSS’s twenty-nine days for a Mexican. Fair Haven Community Health Care, in New Haven, sees anyone and publishes no wait, so it books after New Haven’s, fifteen American cities’ average of twenty-four days, and bills a Yale Health member as uninsured. Ireland publishes no wait, and a visit there comes when it comes. A visit kept can end in a referral, as often as doctors there refer, and the specialist is seen after the published wait: a month in America, twelve weeks in England, eleven in Ontario and twenty in British Columbia. In England a prescription is £9.90 an item under 60, and a prepayment certificate, £32.05 for three months or £114.50 for a year, makes every one free while it runs: Prepay Three Months or Prepay A Year. An emergency keeps you in the department its median time where you live, New York’s 204 minutes or Ireland’s nearly six hours, and you miss what that time held. In America, Your Pharmacy fills a generic at its own cash price, your plan’s copay never more: Walmart’s $4 against the $40 median. A long-term condition is reviewed at NICE’s interval, with a doctor’s visit booked for it. Its medicine is a supply filled where you live with a week left, 28 days in England and 30 in America; Fill A Longer Supply takes up to 56 days in England, 90 in America and 100 in Ontario. A course of CBT is NICE’s eight weekly sessions at the practice’s own price, or free on the NHS in England after its six weeks. Done, it lifts depression at the published rate, NHS Talking Therapies’ 48.5% or a private course’s 36%, and it no longer weighs on you.
