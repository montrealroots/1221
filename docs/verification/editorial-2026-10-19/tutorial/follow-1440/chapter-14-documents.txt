Passport And ID, on Papers, keeps your passport and your ID, and Permission To Work keeps your student status and what comes after it. Each paper you hold opens where it’s listed: who issued it, the last digits of its number, when it was issued and when it runs out, and what it lets you do today. A passport comes from the country you’re a citizen of, at its office’s fee and in its office’s time. Under Your Passport, press Apply For One, or Renew It, and where the office sells a faster service, Apply Faster. Applying starts it: you hold the new passport only once it’s issued, and it cancels the old one, with any travel authorization issued on it. Your ID is the state’s ID card where you live. Under Your ID, press Get with the card’s name to apply for one at its fee. Connecticut mails it, and it’s a photo ID once it arrives. A new bank asks for a photo ID in force, a passport, a license or an ID card, before it takes you on. A license that runs out isn’t renewed in SLATE, so an ID card or a passport takes its place. Report Lost cancels a passport, a license, an international driving permit or an ID card, and Report Stolen does the same for one that was stolen. A lost passport takes what was issued on it with it, all but an F-1. A lost license is replaced where it was issued for someone living there, or by mail from out of state where the issuer sends one, as New York does. In New York it’s free if it was stolen and the police have your report: File A Police Report, online where the police take one, New Haven’s among them. A lost ID card is replaced with Replace It where it was issued, for someone living there, at the issuer’s fee, and it comes after the issuer’s published weeks. Going Abroad says what each country asks of your citizenship: free to enter, a travel authorization first, or a visa. Pick a country to see what it asks and what you’re missing. There’s no travel in SLATE, so it’s a rule to read, not a form to fill in. A visit is only a visit: staying, or working, needs a different permission. A student on an F-1 holds US status while they study and for sixty days after the program ends. After the degree, Apply For OPT, on Permission To Work, gives you twelve months to work in your field, lost after 90 days in all without work. Residence Abroad says what another country asks before you can make a home there. Next, “Money” shows everything you have and owe. The Details Some countries ask your passport to last months past the day you leave. Where an office’s own fee from abroad couldn’t be found, as with New Zealand’s GST, the fine print says so and the fee is estimated. For anyone not a citizen, an ID card’s term is estimated too, since the state publishes none, and Your ID says so. Replacing a lost ID card costs the issuer’s fee: New York’s $8, Connecticut’s $30, California’s $40 and free from 62, Ontario’s $10, British Columbia’s $15, the INE’s nothing, and €35 for Ireland’s passport card.
