The Calendar panel at the top of Home shows what’s coming up: the next thing you need to be at, how soon it is, and up to five more after it. Press an entry’s name or its time to see when it starts, how long it lasts, where it is and what missing it would cost. Anything you need to be at is a commitment: a class, a rehearsal call, a work-study shift, an appointment, a plan of your own. When the clock reaches one, it stops, and a dialog with the commitment’s name asks what you want to do. Press Go to be there. Going takes as long as the commitment lasts, and the clock runs on through it. Stand Here closes the dialog and keeps the clock paused at the commitment. Only a plan of your own has a Skip It button, which lets you walk away from it. You can’t skip a class or any other commitment, so press Go, or Stand Here to wait. It happens the next time the clock moves: you’re there if you’re awake, out of hospital and in its town, and if not, you miss it. The clock hasn’t moved yet, so nothing has come due.
