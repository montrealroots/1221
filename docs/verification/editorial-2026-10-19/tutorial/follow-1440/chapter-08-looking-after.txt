Press Look After Yourself, in the top bar, for everything a day needs besides sleep: Eat A Meal, Order In, Shower, Work Out and Unwind. Each line shows, before you press it, how long it takes, what it costs and what it would change. What matters most right now is at the top. Anything that costs you more every hour you leave it comes first (hunger, going unwashed, stress past its limit), then whatever would do most for your readiness. The top line is marked Needed Now or Most Worth It. Your groceries are bought each day at your town’s prices, and you eat through them during the day. Eat A Meal feeds you and lifts your morale, more the hungrier you were. Order In does the same without the cooking, at the price of a delivery. Shower gets you clean and eases stress. Unwind eases stress and lifts your morale. Work Out builds fitness (your Stamina) and eases stress. It costs energy, uses up some of what you’ve eaten and leaves you needing a shower. On a dry, mild day it’s a run, which builds a little more than a workout indoors. If you can’t do something right now, it’s grayed out with the reason. Read “Your Own Plans” next, to put something of your own on the calendar. The Details Your weight follows what you eat against what your body burns. A meal ordered in counts as a restaurant’s larger one, and a workout or a show burns more. As the years go by your body burns less, so the same meals put weight on. A heavier body is more likely to develop diabetes, high blood pressure or arthritis, and to die sooner. You can get hurt on a run, in a workout or on stage, or on an ordinary day. A sprain or a pulled muscle stops you training until it heals. A broken bone or a concussion keeps you out of classes too, as a stay in hospital does.
