Your birthday comes every year, at nine in the morning wherever you are. The age you turn changes how far your body can go and how much a night gives back. A life ends. Each year carries the chance a real year at your age carries, from the US life table, made lighter or heavier by the life expectancy where you live, for the fourteen countries it’s known for, heavier for a body worn down and lighter for one kept well. The game never says when. When it ends, the clock stops for good, and the save shows who you were, what you earned and what you left behind. It stays in its slot until you delete it.
