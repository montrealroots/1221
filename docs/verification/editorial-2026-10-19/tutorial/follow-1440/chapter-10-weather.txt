The Weather panel on Home shows the weather now and for the next few days. Press today’s weather, or any day, to see more: the week, each day and each hour. The weather comes from your town’s climate and the season, with some day-to-day luck, and it stays the same however often you look. It affects two things: whether a workout is a run outdoors, and the weeks when the flu goes around. Read “Drama School” next: it’s where your life as an actor begins. The Details Thunderstorms come as often as published counts say for the big cities that have them. Everywhere else they’re estimated from the town’s climate, and they come mostly on hot days.
