Papers holds your health cover, your benefits and pension, your tax returns and your documents. To open it, press the Papers button on the Papers line in Your Affairs on Home, or choose Papers in the menu. The Papers line shows what covers you, any unemployment pay, and anything that needs doing, such as a tax return that’s due. The tabs are Tax Returns, Health Cover, Unemployment, Passport And ID, and Permission To Work. While you’re at school, the Yale Health plan covers you. It’s billed each term, and it pays for a doctor’s visit only at Yale Health Center. Your Cover, on Health Cover, shows what covers you and what it costs. You don’t book a doctor whenever you like. When you’re ill enough to need one, or a long-term condition is due for its review, you get an appointment at your practice after its usual wait, and it goes on your calendar. Your Doctor, on Health Cover, is where you choose the practice. In New Haven, Yale Health Center sees a student it covers the next weekday, at no charge. Go, and you’re seen and billed; miss it, and you aren’t, and Book An Appointment books it again. Each piece of care brings a bill for your share, the part your plan doesn’t pay, under Care And Its Bills. On its due date it’s taken from your checking account, and if there isn’t enough, it becomes medical debt, which Pay It Off settles. If you have insurance, Apply For Assistance puts a Connecticut hospital bill in for the hospital’s financial assistance before it’s due. After school, Yale’s plan covers you to the end of July. From 60 days before it ends, press Enroll, under The Marketplace on Health Cover, to join the cover you qualify for: Medicaid or a state program if your income is low enough, or a marketplace plan. Enrolling isn’t the same as being covered: you’re covered only from the plan’s start date, usually the first of the next month. Drop The Plan ends a plan of your own. A long-term condition such as asthma needs care each month, which you pay for under Your Long-Term Conditions, on Health Cover. Let It Go stops the care, and Keep It Up starts it again. Depression can be lifted by a course of therapy: choose a Therapist where you live and press Begin A Course Of 8 Sessions. If you’re out of paid work, press File A Claim, on Unemployment, to claim unemployment insurance where you live, if your past wages qualify; in Connecticut a claim pays only once you’ve left school. SNAP, on the same tab, puts food assistance on an EBT card for groceries; press its Apply button to ask for it. You claim a state pension on Unemployment too, once your work record and your age allow. Each February, the year’s tax return is ready on Tax Returns if you owe tax, are owed a refund or earned at least the standard deduction, and every year on an F-1. Press File It by mid-April. Any balance is paid from your bank, savings included, and filing late with a balance costs penalties. Whatever your bank couldn’t pay grows until you settle it with Pay. After you report a document lost or stolen, File A Police Report, beside it on Passport And ID, files a report online where the police take reports that way. Report Lost itself opens with the next chapter. Read “Passports And ID” next, about your passport, your ID, and your permission to study and work. The Details Enroll takes you into Medicaid (Medi-Cal in California, HUSKY D in Connecticut), New York’s Essential Plan or Covered Connecticut, with no premium, whenever last month’s income is low enough. Cover starts on the first of the next month (HUSKY D on the first of the month you enroll), or when Yale’s ends, if that’s later. From 2027 Medicaid asks for a month spent partly at the School, or a month with $580 of income (pay, interest or a benefit counts), unless you’re 65 or over, on SNAP under its work rules or, if you ask, in hospital that month. In Connecticut that’s the month before you apply, or any one of the 6 months before a renewal. If you fall short, Apply For HUSKY D brings a notice giving you 30 days from receiving it to answer, and at renewal the notice keeps you covered meanwhile. Request A Hardship Exception answers it with a stay in hospital. Covered Connecticut is for an income over HUSKY D’s line and at least at the poverty line, and from 2027 for a citizen or a green card holder; it ends once Yale Health covers you again. Your program is checked again at renewal, every six months from 2027. If last month’s income now fits the other program, you’re moved there with no gap. If you fit neither, or don’t meet its work rules, it ends at the end of a month, with at least 10 days’ notice, and it also ends if you move out of the state. Otherwise, Enroll buys the marketplace plan in New York, California or Connecticut, starting the same way. You can enroll during open enrollment, in the 60 days before Yale Health ends, or within 60 days of losing cover; dropping a plan or leaving it unpaid doesn’t open that window. Each month you live in California without cover adds California’s penalty to your tax return. A marketplace plan may deny a claim (KFF finds one in five are denied): the care is then billed as if you were uninsured, and Appeal It within 180 days gets a decision within 60. New York and California don’t pay the first week of a claim, and Connecticut does. A week with some work pays less, or nothing, and a week abroad pays nothing. If you live in Britain as a British or Irish citizen, File A Claim is for Universal Credit: assessed a month at a time and paid a week later, it’s the standard allowance, less 55p for each pound earned and something for savings over £6,000, and nothing with savings over £16,000. In Ireland it’s Jobseeker’s Allowance, €254 a week for the days out of work, less something for savings over €20,000. You claim a state pension under Unemployment: Claim Social Security, or Claim It for the UK’s or Ireland’s State Pension, the IMSS pension or the Pensión para el Bienestar. A pension has federal tax withheld each month. You get ill at a young adult’s rates, and catch the flu more often when you’re short of sleep or stressed, in the weeks your town’s climate brings it. A Connecticut hospital takes its financial assistance off an uninsured patient’s bill straight away. With insurance, Apply For Assistance makes the bill free if your income is up to 250% of the poverty line, and 67% off up to 550%. Buy Dental adds a dental plan of your own where your health plan pays nothing for the dentist, in New York or alongside a Covered California plan; Drop Dental ends it, though Yale’s, paid for the year, runs to its end. On a 1040-NR with no wages, the return is due by June 15, though Connecticut’s return is still due in mid-April. UK tax is settled separately for each UK tax year, by the next January 31. The IRS pays a refund 21 days after you file, 7 weeks later on a 1040-NR filed without a Social Security number, and a state pays on its own schedule. What your bank couldn’t pay grows from the due date, by the IRS’s 7% a year in interest and a penalty of 0.5% a month, up to 25% of it. The IRS bills it when you file. If you leave it, the IRS writes every five weeks from the due date, the last letter a final notice, and 30 days after that it takes the balance from your bank accounts. If you never file a return that owes tax, the IRS files one for you 365 days after it was due, from what your employers and banks reported. If you file your own within 120 days, it replaces the IRS’s before any of it is assessed; if you file later, the tax becomes what your return says, and the penalty and interest fall in proportion. Where a place publishes how long people wait to see a doctor, your appointment comes after that wait: in England anywhere from the same day to five weeks, four days in New York and thirteen in Los Angeles, the next day for about a quarter of people in Canada (a week otherwise, estimated), and twenty-nine days at IMSS for a Mexican. Fair Haven Community Health Care, in New Haven, sees anyone and publishes no wait, so it books after the average for fifteen American cities, twenty-four days, and bills a Yale Health member as uninsured. Ireland publishes no wait, so a visit there isn’t booked ahead. A visit can end in a referral, as often as doctors there refer, and you see the specialist after the published wait: a month in America, twelve weeks in England, eleven in Ontario and twenty in British Columbia. In England a prescription costs £9.90 an item if you’re under 60, and a prepayment certificate, £32.05 for three months or £114.50 for a year, makes every one free while it lasts: press Prepay Three Months or Prepay A Year. An emergency keeps you in the department for the median time where you live, 204 minutes in New York or nearly six hours in Ireland, and you miss whatever was on in that time. In America, Your Pharmacy fills a generic at its own cash price, never more than your plan’s copay: $4 at Walmart against a $40 median. A long-term condition is reviewed at NICE’s interval, with a doctor’s visit booked for it. Its medicine is refilled where you live when a week of the supply is left, 28 days’ worth in England and 30 in America; Fill A Longer Supply gets up to 56 days in England, 90 in America and 100 in Ontario. A course of CBT is NICE’s eight weekly sessions at the practice’s own price, or free on the NHS in England, its first session six weeks away. Once it’s finished, it lifts depression as often as published figures say, 48.5% of the time for NHS Talking Therapies and 36% for a private course, and a lifted depression no longer weighs on you.
