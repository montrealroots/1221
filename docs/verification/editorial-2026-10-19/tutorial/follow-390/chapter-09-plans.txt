Press Open Calendar, on Home’s Calendar panel, to see the whole month. Press a day, then Put Something On It. Say what it is, when it starts and how long it lasts, from half an hour to all day, then press Put It On. A plan of your own works like any other commitment: the clock stops at it, and you choose Go or Skip It. You can have two commitments at the same time, and the calendar marks them as a clash. To take a plan off before it starts, press it on the Calendar screen, then Call It Off. If you sleep through a plan, or it comes while you’re in hospital, you miss it. A plan happens in the town where you make it, and nothing in the game takes you away from that town. Read “Weather” next to see the weather in your town.
