# What The Frame Kept, And Why

A sample of the sources the frame area read again and kept, across its files, each with its words and the reason
recorded with its second-pass status (`docs/text/second-pass/frame.json`). Every kept source has a reason of its own
there; these are the ones most worth judging.

| Source | The words | Why they stay |
|---|---|---|
| `src/app/game-context.tsx#CLOCK_FAULT` | "An error in the game stopped the clock. Anything that happened before it stopped still stands. You can try moving the clock again." | It says an error stopped the clock, that what already happened stands (the move isn't rolled back), and that moving the clock again is the way on: every part an error owes the player. |
| `src/engine/core/simulation.ts#FAULT_ROLLED_BACK` | "That didn’t go through because of an error in the game. It changed nothing and charged you nothing, so you can carry on from here." | It says what failed, that nothing changed and nothing was charged, and that the player can carry on, which is true because the command's snapshot is restored. |
| `src/ui/saves/SaveSlots.tsx` (kept within the rewritten source) | "Three slots, three separate lives. What happens in one never touches the others." | A promise the save system keeps, said once on the screen where it matters, in the game's own short rhythm. |
| `src/engine/persistence/local.ts#localStore` | "This browser has no storage SLATE can use, so saves can’t be kept here. Check that this site is allowed to store data." | It names the cause and the one thing the player can check, the site's permission to store data. |
| `src/app/SlateApp.tsx#OPEN_FAILURE` | "That save couldn’t be opened because of an error in the game. Restart SLATE to try again, or restore the slot’s backup if it has one." | It says what failed and why, and both real ways on: the Restart SLATE button right under it, or the slot's backup. |
| `src/engine/world/bootstrap.ts#WEALTH_PROFILE` | "Money isn’t the constraint. Time and access are." / "You didn’t earn this, and everyone in the room knows it." | One line of character for each family, said to the player in their own terms, before the figures that follow it in the same callout. |
| `src/engine/content/personality-traits.ts#PERSONALITY_TRAITS [light_sleeper]` | "A door, a car, a thought. Anything wakes you and then that’s the night gone." / "You’re never quite rested, and it accumulates." | A light sleeper in the images one knows, and its cost in five words: a character's description, not a control. |
| `src/engine/content/personality-traits.ts#PERSONALITY_TRAITS [resilient]` | "Illness, injury, a bad year: all of it clears faster in you than in the people around you." / "None. This one is simply luck." | It says plainly that this trait has no cost, where every other trait's card names one. |
| `src/ui/looks/attention.tsx#decisionSentence` | "Answer it by March 3, 2027. That’s in 2 days." / "You dismissed it from Home 3 hours ago. Dismissing only takes it off the list: what it was about still stands, and its date is March 3, 2027." | One sentence for each state of a question, true at the moment, and the dismissed one warns that dismissing settles nothing. |
| `src/engine/systems/life.ts#lookAfterRefusal` | "You aren’t hungry." / "You’re too tired to train. Sleep first." / "Not with the flu: rest until it passes." | Each says why the action is gray in the few words a person would say, with the next step where there is one. |
| `src/engine/systems/life.ts#wellbeingSummary` (the lines kept) | "Running on empty. You need sleep before anything else." / "Rested, steady and ready for a real day." | How You Are's summary in a voice, with the first thing to do where something's wrong. The morale line, which said "the work", was rewritten. |
| `src/ui/shell/GameShell.tsx#SaveIndicator` | "Saved", "Saving…", "Not Saved Yet", "Save Failed: Try Again" | Four states of one button, each a word or two, and the failure says the way on. |
| `src/engine/world/plans.ts` (the calendar.skip refusal) | "It hasn’t started yet. To drop it, open it on the Calendar and press Call It Off." | It names the screen and the button that do what the player was trying to do; the guide quotes it word for word. |
| `src/engine/content/languages.ts` (154 sources) | "Afrikaans", "Māori", "Scottish Gaelic", … | The English names of the world's languages by their ISO codes, spelled as English speakers spell them, Māori with its macron. |
| `src/engine/decided.ts` (27 sources) | "Whether a US bank turns away somebody with a standing ChexSystems report." | Developer notes on each random draw, read only by `tests/deciders.test.ts` and `tools/deciders.mjs`: no file the game ships imports `decided.ts`. |
| `src/engine/content/guide/start.ts#START [optional]` | "The weather beside it shows the same town, hour by hour." / "Go There on a notice takes you to its screen and tab." | Two short lines under Worth Doing With It, each one fact the player can use. |
| `src/engine/systems/life.ts#commitmentWithin` | "Classes starts at 9:00 AM: there isn’t the time before it." | It names the commitment and its time, and the reason; two other areas' guide entries and eleven tests quote it word for word, and it reads as speech. |
