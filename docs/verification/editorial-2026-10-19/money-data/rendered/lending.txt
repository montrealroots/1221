=== us-chase (the life banks at us-chase)
Overdraft
How This Works 
Chase pays what the account can’t without an arrangement, and charges $34.00 for each item that overdraws it, at most 3 a day, unless the account is back by the end of the next business day, and nothing while it’s overdrawn by $50 or less, and none on an item of $5.00 or less. Payments at the same moment post largest first. It covers up to about $500.
You haven’t opted in, so an everyday debit card purchase the account can’t cover goes on the card, or is declined, and costs no fee. Other payments, like autopay and standing orders, go into the overdraft either way. The law requires a US bank to ask you first.
Opt In To Debit Card Overdrafts 
Sources And Estimates
Source: chase.com standard overdraft practice; Chase Debit Card Coverage, opted into or not; Chase Overdraft Assist, chase.com overdraft FAQs: no fee overdrawn by $50 or less at the end of the business day, or brought back to that by 11 PM ET the next business day; Chase Deposit Account Agreement effective June 14, 2026: no fee on an item of $5 or less, debits posted by time, ties highest first. How far the bank lets the account go below zero is estimated: it publishes no limit.

=== nz-bnz (the life banks at nz-bnz)
Overdraft
How This Works 
BNZ arranges no overdraft on this account, but pays what it can’t, and charges NZ$2.00 for a month it’s more than NZ$20 overdrawn, or nothing if you’re under 19. The bank charges interest too, at a rate it doesn’t publish, so the game charges none. It covers up to about NZ$500.
Sources And Estimates
Source: BNZ Personal account, service, and facility fees, March 31, 2026: on YouMoney an unarranged overdraft fee of $2 a Bank Month when more than $20 in debit, none under 19; interest at the Personal Overdraft Base Rate plus 10%, a base rate BNZ doesn’t publish. How far the bank lets the account go below zero is estimated: it publishes no limit.
