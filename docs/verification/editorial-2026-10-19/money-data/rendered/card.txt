How This Works 
You can apply to any bank here, not only yours. It looks at your credit record and whether you have income or money to repay from, and if it says yes, the card arrives a week later. Applying takes a few points off your credit record, whether or not you’re approved. Credit bureaus look at how much of all your cards’ limits you use, taken together.
