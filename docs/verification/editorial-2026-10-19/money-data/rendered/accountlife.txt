=== fr-lcl (the life banks at fr-lcl)
Your Accounts Over Time
Term deposits, round-ups, accounts left unused, and canceling something new.
How This Works 
Round-Ups, System’Épargne. Every card payment in euros is rounded up to the next 1 € or the next 5 €, as you choose, and the total moves to your savings twice a month.
Round Up To Choose… The next €1.00 The next €5.00 
Round-Ups: Off How This Works 
Not now: Choose what to round up to: €1.00, €5.00.
When the account is short: If the period’s transfer would leave the account in debit, it’s canceled in full for that period, not carried.
What The Bank Leaves Open, And What The Game Leaves Out
The day of the twice-monthly transfer: the terms say each “quinzaine”, so the game moves it on the 1st and the 16th.
A payment already at a whole euro (or a multiple of 5 €): the game moves nothing.
Turned off, the round-ups counted before aren’t credited, as the terms say; switching your primary account to another bank drops them too.
Not in the game: A deferred-debit card’s round-ups move at the month’s end; the game’s card is an immediate-debit one.
Not in the game: A child’s account or an LCL-chosen charity can receive them; the game moves them to your savings.
