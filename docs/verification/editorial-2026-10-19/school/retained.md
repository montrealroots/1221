# What The School Area Kept, And Why

A sample of the sources this pass kept as they were, with their words and the reason recorded for each, so the owner can
judge the reasons as well as the rewrites. Every kept source has its reason in `docs/text/second-pass/school.json`.

## Batch 1

| Source | Words | Why it stays |
|---|---|---|
| `systems/coursework.ts#mateBusy` | “Ethan is in class until three.” “Ethan won’t rehearse before 7 in the morning or after 10 at night.” “Ethan has rehearsal calls from three, Tuesday to Friday.” | A classmate's own reason they can't rehearse at the hour you asked, in a short sentence with the hours said as a person says them. |
| `systems/coursework.ts#peerNote` | “Priya says you were on book most of the time, and they couldn’t catch your eye.” “Theo isn’t sure what you’re playing for: your choices weren’t clear to them.” | A scene partner's comment after a rehearsal, in their own voice, naming the one thing of your preparation they'd notice across the room. |
| `systems/coursework.ts#revisitWords` | “You’ve worked on this scene before. It was set on September 1, 2026, and you played Lear with Zoe Park. This time you play the other part.” | A scene coming back says when it was set, which part you played and with whom, then the one thing different this time and how to do it. |
| `systems/coursework.ts#BAND_LABEL` | Unready, Working, Strong, Exceptional | The four bands, each one word, the same word on the card, the notices, Your Notes and the term review. |
| `systems/coursework.ts#ORIENTATION [tour]` | The Building, The Studios And The Library | The session's name lists the three places it shows you, the same studios and library the step refusals send you back to it for. |
| `systems/coursework.ts#answerRefusal` | “You didn’t answer in time.” “You have Voice I then, so you’ll have to say no.” | Plain refusals for answering a partner, and a clash said with what's on your calendar and what that means for the answer. |
| `systems/courserecord.ts#makeUpRefusal` | “Its deadline is at 5:00 PM, and there isn’t time for a session before then.” | A refusal that gives the deadline to the minute, so the player knows exactly what was missed. |
| `systems/evaluation.ts#judgeExact` | Down With The Flu, An Injury Still Mending, A Concussion Still Mending | Short Title Case names for what held a piece back on the day, shown in the work's look and made a sentence by the teacher's note. |
| `ui/components/CourseworkPane.tsx#FeedbackBlock` | “Feedback: Unready, 22 Of 100 · Still There” … “Simulated feedback for Acting I (taught by Gregory Wallace). The words are the game’s, not the teachers’.” | The header gives the band, the score and where the note stands; the label under it says plainly the words are the game's simulation, not the named teachers'. |
| `systems/classmates.ts#disagreement` | “Priya reads the given circumstances in Trifles another way: “The courtroom during Minnie’s trial”, where you have “A cold winter morning in the dead man’s kitchen, the men upstairs looking for evidence”.” | Another reading set beside yours, both quoted from the answers, so Keep Your Reading and Try Theirs are a real choice between two named answers. |
| `systems/classmates.ts#CHOICES` | Keep Your Reading, Try Theirs, Try Both Next Rehearsal, Help Now, Accept It, Push Back | Short Title Case verb phrases that say the answer itself and are distinct from the buttons beside them. |
| `systems/playreading.ts#researchRefusal` | “Read the play first. You need questions before you can research it.” | The refusal gives the step to take and the reason it comes first. |
