Sources And Estimates
Each way of playing shifts what counts most in your work toward what it is, and uses the abilities shown. It moves the balance without adding to it, so at full ability every choice that fits reaches 100; below that, a choice that leans on what you do well shows you at your best. A choice the material or the director doesn’t take holds the qualities it leans on to 85% of themselves. A choice that fits can still suit the scene or go against it, by what the scene is (its genre, its reader, its tones and turns, its features and language, its circumstances, the medium and the reading you’ve taken): one that suits it is easier to make work and needs less rehearsal, one that goes against it is harder and needs more, and at full ability either reaches 100. Judged movement by movement, the piece only counts stamina as tiring late in it, however it’s played, so no choice uses it here. The sizes are estimated.
