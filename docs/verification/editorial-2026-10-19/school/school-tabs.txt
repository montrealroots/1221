Sources And Estimates
A mandatory medical leave (p.159) is recommended by Yale Health and decided by the dean, on an individual assessment of risk and of the modifications the School can make; you can appeal in writing within 7 days, and the leave stands while it’s heard. What starts an assessment in the game is how you’re managing (stays in hospital, class days missed ill), never a diagnosis; where the Bulletin is silent, the rest of its process is the game’s own. The Bulletin’s leave (pp.157–160): one, two or three terms, personal after a term completed and current with requirements and normally not after the tenth day, medical on a Yale Health physician’s recommendation, no aid, Yale Health’s student cover ending the day it’s granted or back to the insurance term’s start before classes, Affiliate cover at $5,416 a term, notice eight weeks before return, and dismissal for not returning. The game’s own rules: a leave’s length must return you in the season you left, since most acting courses run fall to spring; five working days to decide; the physician’s view read from care, injury or illness in the last thirty days; the rebate schedule applied to the living scholarship. An F-1 student’s personal leave ends their status and needs travel the game doesn’t have, so it’s refused, with the reason. Used: 0 of 3 terms. Sources And Estimates
Each way of playing shifts what counts most in your work toward what it is, and uses the abilities shown. It moves the balance without adding to it, so at full ability every choice that fits reaches 100; below that, a choice that leans on what you do well shows you at your best. A choice the material or the director doesn’t take holds the qualities it leans on to 85% of themselves. A choice that fits can still suit the scene or go against it, by what the scene is (its genre, its reader, its tones and turns, its features and language, its circumstances, the medium and the reading you’ve taken): one that suits it is easier to make work and needs less rehearsal, one that goes against it is harder and needs more, and at full ability either reaches 100.
