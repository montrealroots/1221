
===== seagull-4 =====
What The Seagull AsksEveryday Speech
Timing: Some
Physical Work: Some
Playing Off The Partner: Built On It. Nina and Konstantin play every line off each other: his pleading, her refusals, her memory of him.
Vocal Demand: Some
A Private Exchange: Much. Konstantin’s study at night, the storm outside: two people who were young together, alone.
Tone: grave to lyric to grave, 5 changes of tone to play. She breaks into the lines of his old play (“Men, lions, eagles…”) between bouts of present despair.
