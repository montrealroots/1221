## Area A: Where A Life Comes From, Kept Apart

The brief: "Resolve the `create.livesWhereBorn` limitation ... Separate these concepts correctly: Birthplace. Citizenship.
Previous residence. Current residence. Banking history. Work authorization. Birthplace alone must not grant residency, an
existing bank account, citizenship, or work eligibility."

**`create.livesWhereBorn`, retired.** It was form state on the config, never on the person: Place Of Birth wrote it and
Starting Point's I Still Live Where I Was Born drew it only for a life outside the School, which creation never begins.
Nothing in a save read it. It went: creation no longer writes or draws it, `newLifeFrom` drops it from an old draft, and a
life outside the School carries Where You Live with the birthplace only where the two were the same place, read from the
places themselves. Its effects-matrix row became `create.citizenshipId`'s.

**Each concept, where the save keeps it and what reads it:**

| Concept | Kept | Read by |
|---|---|---|
| Birthplace | `Person.homeRegionId` (Identity's Place Of Birth) | the body's drawing; the citizenship it suggests (Same As Your Place Of Birth), never applies |
| Citizenship | `Person.citizenshipId`, asked on Identity from every country that isn't another's territory (`citizenships()`) | the passport, the F-1 visa, America's permission to work (`usWorkAuthorized`), US-person rules at banks (`homeaccount.ts`), America's tax residency |

| Banking history | `homeBankId`, offered only where Before Yale names a place abroad and that bank keeps an account for its customer's case | `populateWorld`'s opening; the account's own rules after |
| Work authorization | the documents: citizenship, the F-1, OPT (`permission.ts`) | every paid job, by `usWorkAuthorized` |

**What changed, measured** (`tests/b27-creation-origins.test.ts`, seven tests): born in New York and a citizen of Japan,
a Japanese passport, an F-1 and no right to work in America by birth; born in Tokyo and an American, an American passport

birthplace with another country chosen it says that nearly everyone born in America is a citizen from birth (the
Fourteenth Amendment). Review says the citizenship back.

**A reversal, said plainly.** On 2026-09-22 the owner directed that the place of birth decide citizenship ("whatever you
put your place of birth it generates what country citizenship you're under"), and the migration from 82 reset every
citizenship to the birthplace's on every opening. Brief 27 says the opposite in as many words ("Birthplace alone must not
grant ... citizenship"), and it is the later instruction, so it was followed. The migration now gives a citizenship only
to a person with none anybody holds (the birthplace's suggestion), and keeps one that disagrees with the birthplace.

**An assumption kept, and an outstanding decision.** A US citizen who says nothing on Before Yale still holds, at the
published share, the license of the state they were born in: the game's assumption that a US-born citizen who didn't say
otherwise grew up there (`documents.ts`, `startingDocuments`). Without it, a US citizen with no passport (more than half,
at the published share) would start with no photo ID at all, which nobody of 22 at Yale has. It is the one place the
birthplace still stands in for a residence, and only where the player said nothing; whether creation should require a
previous residence of every life instead is put to the owner (`CHECKPOINT.md`, Outstanding Decisions). NPCs' citizenships
are still their birthplaces': a simulation shortcut, as no NPC is a Person (only the player is).
