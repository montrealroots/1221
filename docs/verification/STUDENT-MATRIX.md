## Statuses

- **Behaviorally Verified**: Every acceptance assertion’s test is in its file by title, and every command is registered.
- **Implemented**: Its commands are registered, but an acceptance assertion’s test isn’t found.
- **Simplified With A Boundary**: Verified, and its boundary says where SLATE stops short of the source.
- **Blocked**: Can’t be built in scope; the row says what blocks it.
- **Deferred**: One of the owner’s 16 deferred items, outside the counts.

## Basis

- **documented**: The rule is the institution's or the law's, read in a cited source.
- **simplified**: The rule follows a cited source, with a stated simplification.
- **design**: Original SLATE design, labelled as such where the player meets it.
- **uncertain**: The source doesn't settle it; SLATE's labelled assumption.
