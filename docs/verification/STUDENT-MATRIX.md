## Gaps Found At The Start, And What Became Of Them

From the audits checked in beside this file (`school-journey-audit.json`, `papers-bank-audit.json`) and the research. Each row whose goal had a gap:

- **Register for the program.** Registration changed nothing (school-journey-audit low). Fixed: registration gates classes, shifts and the SSN.
- **Meet the class and the faculty, and learn what the School asks.** The faculty session named teachers and changed nothing. Fixed: it sets out the policies, kept readable on the Standing tab.
- **Run lines with a classmate.** A session trained whichever scene was weakest, often another partner's. Fixed: shared scenes only, chosen, said.
- **Pass each course.** No course had a record; the degree came with time in standing. Fixed: per-course Pass/Incomplete/Fail and make-up.
- **Take the labs: Drama 50, the New Play Lab, You Are The Creator.** Missed labs never met any rule beyond attendance. Decided and implemented: Pass/Fail courses.
- **Join a Yale Cabaret show.** The warning ban was checked only at joining. Fixed.
- **Leave the School: withdraw, or be dismissed.** Withdrawal didn't exist; dismissal borrowed withdrawal's grace. Fixed.
- **Graduate.** Graduation checked no course. Fixed.
- **Close a current account.** Pending items never blocked closing (sign error); a Citi close past its limit failed halfway. Fixed.
- **Be told which account needs attention.** The low-balance alert watched the primary only. Fixed.
- **Lock, report and renew each bank's debit card.** Only the primary account's card could be managed. Fixed.
- **Get a Social Security number on an F-1.** No taxpayer number existed. Added.
- **Hold, lose and replace identity documents.** A lost passport canceled the F-1; a replacement ID kept the theft record. Fixed.
- **File the year's return and pay what's owed.** An F-1 student filed as a resident. Fixed.
- **Be compensated for an injury at school work.** New York's rules applied in New Haven. Fixed.
