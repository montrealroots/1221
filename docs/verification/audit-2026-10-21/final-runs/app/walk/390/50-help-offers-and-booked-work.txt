Offers And Booked Work
Show Less

An offer says the role, the pay, the dates and how long it stands, three days. Press Accept The Offer to book it, or Decline The Offer. Accepting puts each work day on your calendar and promises the pay for each week of it.

Each work day stops the clock: press Go to be there. Each is judged, and the part goes on your professional credits.

Where To Find It
Career, Opportunities, Offers.
When You Can Use It
From the guide’s chapter “Finding Work”, once casting makes you an offer.
Show Me Where
What To Press
Open Career, then Opportunities, and read the offer under Offers, or under What Needs You on Home.
Press Accept The Offer or Decline The Offer before it lapses.
Under Booked Work, prepare the part with the same three steps as the sides.
On each work day, press Go when the clock stops for it.
What You Need First
Permission to work in the US for every day of it, as Papers shows it.
No other booking on the same days, and in the summer no School call on them.
TIME AND MONEY
Time
Each work day’s hours, with the trip each way
Energy
8 a work day, as a School rehearsal call takes, on top of its hours awake (estimated)
Money
None: the work pays you
What Happens
STRAIGHT AWAY
The work days go on your calendar, and the pay for each week is promised, due the Thursday after it. It isn’t paid yet.
LATER ON
A stage part goes on your credits at its first night; a screen part when it’s shot, and as released once it comes out, when its footage can go on a reel.
Miss a shoot day, or a second day of a run, and the production replaces you.
READING THE RESULT
Promised pay is what the contract owes you, not money you have yet. A student film’s pay is deferred: it’s paid only if the film earns money later, and no film in the game does, so nothing is paid.
How It Connects
COMES FIRST
Permission To Work: An offer can be accepted only where your permission to work covers every one of its days.
CHANGES LATER
The Calendar: Accepting puts each work day on your calendar, and each stops the clock for you.
KEPT IN YOUR RECORD
Your Résumé: The part goes on your professional credits, and your résumé reads as behind until it has it.
WHAT LEADS HERE
Auditions And Callbacks: Casting reads your audition’s band, with the role’s fit and the field, when it decides whether to offer you the role.
Work While You’re At The School: An offer in term is accepted on the School’s approval, or against it with the warning said first.
What To Do Next
Update your résumé once the part is on your credits.
If You Can’t
Your F-1 lets you work only on campus while you’re a student.
You’re booked for another job on those days.
The School holds you for one of its calls on those days.
The offer lapsed.
MISTAKES, AND PUTTING THEM RIGHT
Sleeping through a work day. A missed day of a shoot ends the job.
What Decides It
Permission To Work
Every day of the work, against the papers you hold
The Part
Its preparation, your abilities and techniques, and the day’s condition, judged as every piece of work is
A Worked Example

An offer of a two-day shoot at $125.00 a day, estimated, standing three days.

Press Accept The Offer.
Prepare the part, then press Go on each shoot day.

What comes of it. Two shoot days go on your calendar and $250.00 is promised for that week. After the second day the part is on your credits as not yet released.
