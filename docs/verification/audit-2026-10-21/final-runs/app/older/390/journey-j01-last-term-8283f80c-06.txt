Term deposits, round-ups, accounts left unused, and canceling something new.

How This Works

The bank’s terms: A checking or savings account you haven’t used yourself for 24 months may become inactive; a CD counts as inactive from when it’s funded until it matures or you take money out early. After 30 months it may become dormant: no debit card purchases, transfers, deposits or withdrawals, no online or mobile banking, and the debit card is restricted. After a longer period set by state law, it may be closed and the money handed to the state as unclaimed property, with a letter first.
