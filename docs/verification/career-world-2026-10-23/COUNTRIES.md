For anyone without the country’s citizenship; applied for ahead, with the production’s support. Source S01, https://immi.homeaffairs.gov.au/visas/getting-a-visa/visa-listing/temporary-activity-408/entertainment-activities, read 2026-10-23, the page last updated 2026-09-24.

| contract | verified | You need a contract for the work first. | “You must have a contract for your performance or work in Australia.” |
| supporter | verified | Applying from outside Australia for less than three months, you need a supporter: here, the production. | “You must have a supporter to apply for this visa.” |
