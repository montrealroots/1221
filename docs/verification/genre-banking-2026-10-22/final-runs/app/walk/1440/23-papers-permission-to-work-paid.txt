OPT

Form I-765, (c)(3)(B), after your degree

How This Works

OPT is permission to work in your field after your degree, and it keeps your US status going. Each day of professional work you’re booked for counts as a day worked. An approved OPT ends once you’ve had more than 90 days without work in total, and your US status ends with it.

It costs $470.00. Premium processing costs $1,780.00 more and gets you a decision within 30 business days. OPT follows a completed degree: if school ends without yours, it’s denied when it’s decided, and the fee isn’t refunded.

COUNTRY YOUR STATUS THERE 
Canada Canada has no route for a freelance actor. Its Self-Employed Persons Program is paused until further notice, and Express Entry has no category for the arts. 

Ireland Ireland has no route for a freelance actor, because an employment permit needs an employer’s offer. 

Mexico Mexico grants temporary residence on savings of MXN 1,344,373 or an income of MXN 79,771 a month over six months, with the card at MXN 11,140.74 a year and the consulate’s visa at US$56. Moving abroad isn’t part of the game, so you can’t get Mexico’s papers here.
