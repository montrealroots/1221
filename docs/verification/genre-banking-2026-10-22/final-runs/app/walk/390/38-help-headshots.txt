Headshots
Show Less

Headshots are the photographs every submission starts with. A session with a photographer takes four hours and costs about $700.00, both estimated, paid from your bank.

The panel says when yours were taken, where they came from and what’s in them. Headshots more than two years old read as out of date, which is estimated too.

Where To Find It
Career, Portfolio, Headshots.
When You Can Use It
From the guide’s chapter “Your Career”.
Show Me Where
What To Press
Open Career, then the Portfolio tab.
Under Headshots, read the session’s time and price on the button.
Press Book A Headshot Session. The clock moves on while it runs, and the price comes out of your bank.
What You Need First
Free for the session’s four hours, with nothing on your calendar in that time.
Daytime: a session starts between 9 AM and 4 PM.
Enough in your bank for the price.
TIME AND MONEY
Time
four hours (estimated)
Money
$700.00 (estimated)
What Happens
STRAIGHT AWAY
The clock moves on by the session, the price comes out of your bank as a work cost, and your new headshots are on the panel, dated today.
LATER ON
Two years on, they read as out of date, and What’s Missing says so.
READING THE RESULT
Current means they were taken within two years. Out of date means they may not look like you now.
How It Connects
CHANGES STRAIGHT AWAY
The Career Screen: Once you have headshots, What’s Missing stops asking for them, until they’re more than two years old.
Your Accounts: The session’s price comes out of your bank as a work cost.
WHAT LEADS HERE
The Career Screen: What’s Missing names headshots first when you have none, since every submission starts with one.
The Agent And How To Reach Him: A check refuses an approach with no headshots, since every submission starts with one.
Finding Work And Submitting: A submission starts with your headshots: without them it can’t be sent.
What To Do Next
Make your résumé: a submission sends both.
If You Can’t
Photographers work by day: a session starts between 9 AM and 4 PM.
Your bank can’t cover the session.
You’re busy then, or something on your calendar starts before the session would end.
MISTAKES, AND PUTTING THEM RIGHT
Booking one late in the day. A session has to start by 4 PM.
What Decides It
Out Of Date
Headshots taken more than two years before (estimated)
A Worked Example

A free afternoon at 1 PM, with $700.00 or more in the bank.

Open Career, then Portfolio.
Press Book A Headshot Session.

What comes of it. The clock moves to 5 PM, $700.00 comes out of your bank, and Headshots shows them taken today, current.
