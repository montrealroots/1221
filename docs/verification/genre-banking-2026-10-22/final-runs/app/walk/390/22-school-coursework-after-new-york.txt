DRAM 793b · Due May 8, 2029 · Skills: Scene Study, Audition Technique, Character Work

How This Works
Office Hours, an original scene written for SLATE (2026), an empty chemistry classroom after parent-teacher night, 2024: you play The Father, Isaiah Donovan plays The Teacher. A father who works nights comes to see his son’s chemistry teacher, after the last of the other parents have gone. His son is two points below the grade that keeps him on the basketball team, and the teacher can offer one makeup lab, if the father will make sure it gets done.
An Original Scene, Written For SLATE. The facts its Table Work answers rest on:
The son has a 58 in chemistry, and anything under 60 costs him his place on the basketball team.
He turned in none of the last five lab reports.
The father works nights and missed the two emails the teacher sent.
Final grades go in on Friday, and one makeup lab would bring the grade above 60.
The teacher taught the boy’s older sister, who is at college on a scholarship.

DRAM 793b · Due May 8, 2029 · Skills: Scene Study, Audition Technique, Character Work

How This Works
Two Time Zones, an original scene written for SLATE (2026), his girlfriend’s apartment, the last night of his visit, spring 2023: you play June, Julian Kowalski plays Sam. After two years a four-hour flight apart, a couple promised each other at New Year that one of them would move by summer. On the last night of his visit, with a flight at six, she sells her city, he sells his, and neither will say the one thing the other is waiting for.
An Original Scene, Written For SLATE. The facts its Table Work answers rest on:
They’ve been together three years, the last two of them a four-hour flight apart.
She has been offered a promotion that means staying where she is.
He has a lease that ends in June and a job he can do from home three days a week.
At New Year they agreed that one of them would move by summer.
It’s the last night of his visit, and his flight leaves at six in the morning.
