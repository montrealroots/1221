# The Research Data's Dates: Terms

For `docs/GLOSSARY.md`. A date is a word every area uses, so these rows are the coordinator's to place, in Core.

| Say | What It Means | Instead Of |
|---|---|---|
| **September 26, 2026** | A date, as `formatDate` writes it: the month's name, the day, a comma, the year. A day without a year is "September 30"; a month and year, "September 2026"; a run of days, "August 24 to 28"; a span of months, "January to June 2026". A day kept as a key ('YYYY-MM-DD') is said through `formatDateKey`. | 2026-09-26, 26 September 2026, 26 Sep 2026, Sept 2026, 2026-09, 9/26/2026, 26.09.2026, 30 September |
| **read October 10, 2026** | The day a source was read, after the source in its Source line. | read 2026-10-10 |
| **checked October 13, 2026** | The day a round-up rule was checked against the bank's pages. | checked 2026-10-13 |
| **the bank's own words**, **in its words** | A field the screen quotes as the bank's: its dates stay as the bank writes them ("by 31/01/2027"). | |
