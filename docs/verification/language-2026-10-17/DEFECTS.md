# Brief 14’s Defects

Every defect the brief 14 pass found, from the eight areas, the integration’s passes and the editorial review, in
one index the receipt can count. Written by `defects.py` from `defects.json`; edit the entries there, never this
page, and run the script again.

A defect here is something wrong apart from ordinary rewording: a sentence that misstated the game, a sentence built
from parts that came out wrong, a control or layout fault, or a fault in what the game does, in a test or tool the pass
runs, or in the research data or its builders. Read: every defects.md, coordinator.md, each area's HANDOFF.md, and the
editorial review's batches.md where its defects.md points there. One heading, numbered item, table row or bold item is
one defect, as its record drew it, except where a record names a second fault found beside the first. Two records of one
defect make one entry that names both. A fixed entry gives the commit whose fix stands in the tree (another that fixed
it too under also), the files it changed for it, and what holds it now: a test, a check, or the source as it reads. A
left entry gives its record's reason and who it's for. Read on l14-defects at 1db33e2d, and held to the tree by
defects.py --verify.
