The Boat Shed
The Dispensary Door

Barricaded in a rural vet clinic’s drug room after midnight, a veterinarian has to keep a panicking farmer quiet while she measures out enough sedative for whatever he brought in.

THE SCENE
Where And When
A small veterinary clinic on a farm road, past one in the morning. The farmer called her out to look at something he found dead in his north field and couldn’t put a name to, and brought it in wrapped in a tarp. On her table it opened its eyes. It’s loose in the clinic now, and the two of them are in the drug room with the door shut and a filing cabinet against it. His truck is thirty yards across the yard.
The Two Of You
She’s been his farm’s vet for eight years, and he’s never once taken her advice the first time.
You Play
Dr. Paulina Szabo, forty, the vet, still in scrubs and an apron.
The Reader Plays
Wade Pickett, sixty, the farmer, by the window, who wants to make a run for his truck.
How They Play It
Pressing: playing opposite it, the tape counts responsiveness for more and leans on your adaptability.
The Genre Asks
States At The Limit: Fear, grief or exhaustion at their extreme, sustained, and believed though the threat is often not in the room. A self-tape in it is judged more on believability and execution, and calls on your emotional depth and physical expression. The lines, then rehearsal on your feet: an extreme state has to be found in the body, on lines you no longer think about.
Belief In What Isn’t There: An invented world made concrete: the creature, the machine or the magic played as specifically as a cup of tea. A self-tape in it is judged more on believability and interpretation, and calls on your imagination. The breakdown: the world’s rules have to be worked out before they can be believed.
The creature performer acts in suits with restricted senses; the others play terror of a thing that isn’t on set. This comes from the genre’s research, mostly held with low confidence.
What You Want
Keep him still and quiet until she has the dose drawn and a plan to use it.
What’s In The Way
He’s panicking, he’s sure he can reach the truck, and every time he raises his voice the thing on the other side of the door goes quiet and listens.
WHERE IT TURNS
A whispered argument across the drug room: he insists it’s a coyote, a very big coyote, and she reads him the label of the strongest sedative she has. (Comic and Grave, held at once)
Something tries the door handle, slowly, and they both stop breathing. (Grave)
She works out the dose aloud, by weight, and the weight she has to guess makes the number absurd. (Grotesque and Comic, held at once)
She tells him the plan: she opens the door, he runs for the truck and drives for help, and she won’t hear an argument. (Grave)

3 changes of tone between beats and 2 beats held two ways. Rehearsal is complete at 50% in a beat, up to 15% more in a beat a change leads into, and 8% more in one held two ways.

WHAT THE DISPENSARY DOOR ASKS
Everyday Speech, on camera
Timing: Much. The reader calls the sounds from the corridor at set moments, and she has to freeze on each one and pick the whisper up where she left it.
Physical Work: Much. Play the scene crouched by the drug cabinet with your back to the barricaded door (a wall on your side of the frame will do), drawing a dose into a capped prop syringe from a prop vial. The handle turning is a sound cue: react to it alone.
Playing Off The Partner: Much. He’s pushing to run the whole time, and she has to read when he’s about to bolt and stop him with a whisper.
Vocal Demand: Some
Tone: comic to grave to grotesque, 3 changes of tone to play. An argument held in whispers, a sound that ends it, a dose so large it’s funny, then a plan she won’t argue about.

YOUR READING
How This Works
Measure Twice. She meets the impossible as a vet meets any emergency, and her steadiness with the numbers keeps them both alive. It rests on: She works out the dose by weight and reads him the label of the strongest sedative. Fully prepared, it would come to 51.8 for you now.
Eight Years Of His Stubbornness. She knows this man, and the scene is her fighting his stubbornness one more time, with his life on it. It rests on: He’s never taken her advice the first time, and he’s sure he can reach the truck. Fully prepared, it would come to 52.2 for you now.
