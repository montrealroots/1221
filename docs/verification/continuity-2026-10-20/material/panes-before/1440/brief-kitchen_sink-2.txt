Not Going Back
One More Friday

In a Lancashire mill town in 1960, a young wife asks the rent collector for one more week, without letting him or the woman next door learn where the rent money went.

THE SCENE
Where And When
The front room of a rented house on a cobbled street in a Lancashire mill town, on a Friday morning in 1960. The rent is three weeks behind: Jean has lent it, a week at a time, to her sister, whose husband walked out at Easter, and her own husband, on short time since the layoffs at the dye works, believes it’s paid. The landlord’s letter about eviction came on Tuesday, and she hid it. The walls are thin, and the woman next door is listening. Her late mother’s wedding ring is in her apron pocket.
The Two Of You
She has paid this rent man every Friday for four years and never once been short; he likes her, and it makes his job harder.
You Play
Jean Walsh, twenty-four, with a baby asleep upstairs and her pride intact.
The Reader Plays
Mr. Harold Tunstall, the rent collector, in his fifties, with his book open, who has heard every story.
How They Play It
Steady: playing opposite it asks nothing more than the scene does.
The Genre Asks
Caught Live: Behavior that looks unrehearsed: real listening, the next line taken from what the other person just did. A self-tape in it is judged more on connection and responsiveness, and calls on your listening and responsiveness and adaptability. Rehearsing on your feet: what looks unrehearsed is found by playing it through until nothing is set but the wants.
Unknown regional actors with their own accents, breaking with the educated stage voice. This comes from the genre’s research, mostly held with low confidence.
What You Want
Keep the house for one more week, without telling him where the money went.
What’s In The Way
He has heard every excuse in the street, the landlord wants the house back, and the neighbor will hear whatever she says.
WHERE IT TURNS
She asks him in, offers tea, and keeps her voice bright for next door. (Comic and Grave, held at once)
She tells him her husband’s wages are late from the works, a story he has heard twice this morning. (Grave)
He tells her quietly that the landlord means it this time, and she drops her voice and tells him the truth. (Grave)
She lays her mother’s ring on the table at her side as surety; he won’t take it, marks half the arrears paid from his own pocket, and goes. (Lyric and Grave, held at once)

2 changes of tone between beats and 2 beats held two ways. Rehearsal is complete at 50% in a beat, up to 15% more in a beat a change leads into, and 8% more in one held two ways.

WHAT ONE MORE FRIDAY ASKS
Everyday Speech, on camera
Timing: Some
Physical Work: Some
Playing Off The Partner: Built On It. Everything turns on the rent man: what he has heard, what he believes, and what he decides to do with his own money.
Vocal Demand: Much. She speaks at two volumes: the bright voice for the wall and the woman behind it, and the low one for the truth, with the town’s speech in both.
Tone: comic to grave to lyric, 2 changes of tone to play. A show for the neighbors, a story he has heard, the truth told low, and a kindness she didn’t ask for.
Techniques It Asks: Accents And Dialects. The lines are written in the working-class speech of a Lancashire mill town, its rhythms and its “love” and “ta” among them, and the accent is played as the way Jean talks, with the dignity it’s owed, never as color or a joke.

YOUR READING
How This Works
Pride Before Rent. Jean would give up the house before she’d be pitied, and the scene is her pride giving way an inch at a time. It rests on: She has never once been short, keeps her voice bright for next door, and offers her mother’s ring. Fully prepared, it would come to 52.6 for you now.
Her Sister’s Trouble. Every lie keeps her sister’s trouble off the street, and the truth in the third beat is the first time she has said aloud what she did behind her husband’s back. It rests on: She lent the rent to her sister without her husband knowing, and she drops her voice to tell the truth. Fully prepared, it would come to 53.4 for you now.
