How You Are, on Home, shows three numbers: energy, stress and readiness. Energy is what you have left for today. It runs down every hour you’re awake, faster when you owe sleep, and only sleep brings it back. Stress eases while you still have energy and climbs once it runs out, and money you owe adds to it every day. Readiness is how sharp you’d be for work right now. Behind those three is the rest of your wellbeing: morale, condition, fitness, nourishment, hygiene and the sleep you owe. Readiness comes from your energy, your stress and all of these but fitness. Press Readiness to see the ones costing it most, up to three, and press any of those to see where it stands and what it does to you. Condition is how sound your body is. Illness, going hungry, heavy stress and short nights wear it down, and a night of real rest mends it. Fitness is the same as your Stamina, the ability that keeps you going through a long scene or a night on stage. Neither condition nor fitness is your energy or your health: illness, injury, care and health cover are on Papers. Read “Sleep” next, about how your energy comes back. The Details Age sets how high condition and fitness can go. A body in its twenties barely loses a point a year from its best, and one in its seventies loses more than a point a year. The traits you chose at the start each change how you are by their own amount. So does a long-term condition such as diabetes, if it goes untreated.
