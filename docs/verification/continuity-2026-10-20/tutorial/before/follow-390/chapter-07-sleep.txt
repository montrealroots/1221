Sleep is how you get your energy back. Press Sleep in the top bar, and the Sleep dialog asks how many hours, from half an hour to 14 hours. It suggests a number, from when you next need to be somewhere and how much sleep you owe, and shows when you’d wake. Press the button with the hours on it, such as Sleep 8 Hours, and the clock runs on to when you wake. Not Yet closes the dialog. A night’s sleep gives you energy back, though less the more sleep you owe. More than about five hours of real rest also mends your condition, as far as your fitness and age allow, and less than that wears it down. A night under six hours adds to the sleep you owe. Life goes on while you sleep. Anything on your calendar in those hours happens without you, and the dialog lists it before you press. While you’re at a commitment, Sleep is grayed out, and the top bar says when you’ll be free. Read “Looking After Yourself” next, about eating, washing, working out and unwinding.
