The Calendar panel at the top of Home shows what’s coming up: the next thing you need to be at, how soon it is, and up to five more after it. Press an entry’s name or its time to see when it starts, how long it lasts, where it is and what missing it would cost. Anything you need to be at is a commitment: a class, a rehearsal call, a work-study shift, an appointment, a plan of your own. When the clock reaches one, it stops, and a dialog with the commitment’s name asks what you want to do. Press Go to be there. Going takes as long as the commitment lasts, and the clock runs on through it. Stand Here closes the dialog and keeps the clock paused at the commitment. You can only skip a plan of your own, so there’s no Skip It button for a class or an appointment. If you press Stand Here, the commitment starts the next time the clock moves, whether you press Play, Advance or Sleep. You’re there if you’re awake and out of hospital, and, for anything but your own care, in its town and not laid up by an injury; if not, you miss it. The clock hasn’t moved yet, so nothing has come due. Read “The Clock” next: it opens Play, which starts the clock.
