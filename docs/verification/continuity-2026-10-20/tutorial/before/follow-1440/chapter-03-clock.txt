Press Play, in the top bar, to start the clock, and Pause to stop it. The button always shows what pressing it will do. A new life starts with the clock paused. The clock runs in real time: a minute in the game is a minute for you. While it runs, life goes on around you. Hours pass, you get tired and hungry, and anything that’s due happens. The clock stops by itself at every commitment and waits for your answer, as “What’s Coming” explained. Sleep is the exception: it runs straight through everything in those hours, so the Sleep dialog lists what you’d sleep through before you start.
