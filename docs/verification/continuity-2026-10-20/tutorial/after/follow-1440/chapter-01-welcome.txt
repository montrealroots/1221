In SLATE you live one life, hour by hour, in a real town with its own time, seasons, weather, prices, money and laws. This guide shows you how, one part at a time, and each chapter you read opens the part of the game it explains. You start on Home. The line under the greeting shows the time, the town, its time zone, the season and the weather. Where You Are, further down, tells you more about the town. Underlined words are links: press one to see more. Press the town’s name and its details open just below, with more links inside for its region, its country and its money. The date, the season and the daylight work the same way. Some words only become links once you’ve read the guide chapter that covers them. A panel you can’t use yet says Locked and names the chapter to read. Press its Read About It button to go straight there. When you’ve read a chapter, press I’ve Read It at its foot to open what it covers, listed under What It Opens. Then press Next, which names the chapter after it, to read on, or Try It to close the guide and use what just opened. Later closes the guide without opening anything, and the line at the top of the screen keeps a Read button for the next chapter. Once you’ve read its chapter, you can open each part of your life from Home and from the menu down the left. On a small screen, press Menu to see the menu. Each panel has a How This Works button that explains what the panel does and how it connects to the rest of the game. Press the ? key anywhere for the help’s contents, or the / key to search it. The clock waits while you read the help or this guide. Save & Exit, in the top bar, saves your life and takes you back to the save slots. Press Continue there to pick up at the same moment. A new life starts with the clock paused, so nothing happens until you’re ready. Read “What’s Coming” next to open the Calendar panel.
