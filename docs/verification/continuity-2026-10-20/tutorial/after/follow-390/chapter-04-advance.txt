To jump ahead instead of waiting, press Advance in the top bar and choose how far: One Hour, This Evening (6 p.m.), Tonight (10 p.m.), Tomorrow Morning (7 a.m.), Next Event, Next Monday (9 a.m.) or One Week. Choose An Event opens Skip To Event: pick one of the next fourteen things on your calendar and press Skip To with its name. Everything on the way happens, in order. If the jump would pass any commitments, a dialog called On The Way first says how many: press Advance there to go on, or Cancel to stay where you are. The clock still stops at each commitment to ask what you want to do: Go takes you to it and carries the jump on afterwards, and Stand Here ends the jump there, with the clock paused. Time only moves forward, so you can’t undo a jump. Read “What Needs You” next: it’s where you find out what happened. The Details If you jump more than an hour through the night, your character goes to bed by themselves from midnight. That’s a poorer night than one you choose with Sleep, and it never brings your energy above 74. If you let the clock run, or jump an hour at a time, they stay up with you.
