# The Genre Matrix, 2026-10-16

Every genre in the catalog, written by `tools/genre-identity.ts --md` from the code: what each genre is, the register its
scenes are played in and where that comes from, what the engine reads, the preparation that answers it, and its briefs.
`README.md` says what each column means for the player and how it was proved. Regenerate it with:

    npx vite-node tools/ag-baseline.ts -- --out baseline-then.json   # on the code before, for Nearest Before
    npx vite-node tools/genre-identity.ts -- --md docs/verification/genres-2026-10-16/genre-matrix.md --before baseline-then.json

then put this header back. Briefs are given as size, stakes, the reader's way of playing, the five features (t timing,
ph physical, pa partner, vo vocal, im improvisation) and their readings. "Nearest" is the nearest other genre's profile
and its distance (ag-baseline §3's measure); "(same register)" marks a legitimate overlap. "Its Own Briefs Apart" is the
distance between the genre's own two briefs.
