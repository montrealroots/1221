# Interpretation In Context: Criteria Written Before Tuning, 2026-10-16

The owner's brief "Finish Genre Interpretation, Improve Training Advice, And Prove Long-Term Integration", part 1. These
criteria were written before the model's strengths were chosen, and the strengths were then set against them. None was
loosened after a result. `tools/interpretation.ts` measures each.

What was measured before (genres-2026-10-16/README.md, "What's Still Open"): restrained against expansive moved a take by
the actor's own abilities, the same within a tenth in Nordic noir, melodrama and social realism. The way of playing it
leaned the same dimensions and drew the same abilities in every scene.

## The Criteria

- **I1, mastery.** Every brief, every reading, every combination of sides that fits (no `allows` or direction against
  it): 100 for a mastered, fully prepared actor on a sufficient day. Suitability never caps a fitting choice.
- **I2, no blanket genre rule.** Across genres with two or more briefs, the best-suited side of the scale axis (and of
  the pace axis) differs between the genre's own briefs in at least 20% of them. Across the catalog, each side of each
  axis is the better-suited side in at least 20% of briefs.
- **I3, the interaction is real.** For an even actor at 55 in every ability, fully prepared, the difference restrained
  minus expansive varies across briefs with a standard deviation of at least 1.0 point (measured 0.1 or less before),
  and has each sign in at least 25% of briefs.
- **I4, skill and suitability are separate.** A specialist in what expansive draws on (presence, vocal, physical at 80,
  the rest at 45) does better playing expansive than restrained in at least 25% of the briefs where restrained is the
  better-suited side: a well-executed choice that goes against the grain can still win. And an even actor does better
  with the better-suited side in at least 75% of briefs where the two sides' suitability differs by a band.
- **I5, more than one coherent interpretation.** For an even actor at 55, fully prepared, at least two different
  (reading, way of playing) combinations come within 1.0 point of the best in at least 80% of briefs.
- **I6, preparation paths differ.** A side against the grain asks more rehearsal of the choice before it's complete
  than one with it, and the screen says how much.
- **I7, it's said.** Each side's suitability, its band and the signals it rests on are shown before the choice, and a
  take's result separates what the choice's suitability cost or gave from what the actor's abilities cost.
