## What Holds A Specialist Back (`h4/landscape-probe.ts.txt`)

On the pieces where its ability reads most, each specialist's difference from the even actor was taken apart: the gain
from its one ability at 85 alone, and the loss from the other eleven at 46.8 alone.

1. **A little of everything.** Since phase 2b every piece reads "a little of every ability its dimensions concern"
   before its own demands (`BASE_INPUTS`, `content/tasks.ts`), and the techniques at 50 take their share of every
   dimension. On its best work an ability carries 12% to 17% of what the piece's abilities move, so +35 on it gains
   about 2.7 points and −3.2 on eleven others loses about 2.45. A coin toss: ahead on about half.
2. **The weakest dimension.** The whole is three quarters the weighted mean and a quarter the weakest dimension. For an
   even performer the weakest is a three-way tie between connection, consistency and responsiveness, the three
   dimensions with no technique term, and the piece weighs it at a median 8.7% (4.6% at the tenth percentile), while it
   takes a quarter of the whole. A specialist's lowered abilities lower that dimension, and its one strength rarely
   reaches it, so a quarter of the whole can only lose. Without the term (measured, not adopted) H4 would be 9 of 12.
3. **Presence, discipline and stamina have no work of their own in the catalog.** Presence reads most in front of a
   house and with no partner, and 298 of the 431 pieces are taped briefs, most with a reader. Discipline reads in
   consistency and a long score, stamina only as a drain on the later segments. Discipline's and stamina's specialists
   are behind on every whole night (0 of 23 to 30) under every configuration measured; presence's is ahead on 5 to 11
   of 30 nights and 15 to 22 of 30 castings where the configurations leave it any, and on 1 of about 100 briefs.

## What Was Tried, And Why Nothing Was Kept

Two levers, each a temporary switch kept as `h4/switches.patch` and taken out after (`h4/grid.txt` is every run): the
material's own demands scaled (×1 to ×3), and the weakest dimension weighed by how much the piece asks of it (a
shortfall counting in proportion to its dimension's weight against the average, continuous and monotone, mastery still
100).

- **H4 and H2 pull against each other.** Every configuration that moved H4 past 3 broke H2 on class scenes: the
  listening specialist came out best on half or more of them, as it was on all 56 before phase 2b. The weakest-
  dimension term is what holds listening back on partnered scenes; weighing it by the piece's weights, though better
  founded in itself, breaks H2, which held before.
- **Concentrating the demands crowds out presence**, which falls toward nothing as the others' demands rise.
- **No configuration reaches 10.** Presence, discipline and stamina stay far under 60% throughout, so at most 9 of 12
  could meet even if every other ability did.

So no change was made for H4. The finding stands that the weakest-dimension term reads a three-way tie of the dimensions
with no technique term and weighs it beyond the piece's own weight; it's recorded for the owner, not changed, since
changing it breaks H2.

## For The Owner

H4 is reported as measured, 2 of 12, not met, and isn't counted otherwise. What a decision could rest on:

1. **Keep H4 as the record.** Specialization pays on emotional depth's and listening's work, about breaks even on six
   abilities' best work, and doesn't on presence's, discipline's or stamina's, whose strengths are the house, the run and
   the long night: a third of what the catalog asks.
2. **Give presence, discipline and stamina work of their own**, content rather than weights: solo pieces and direct
   address for presence, long scored pieces for discipline, physically demanding whole nights for stamina, each
   researched and read from its text as the drama scenes were, then measured against H2 and H5.
3. **A corrected H4 (H4′), proposed:** each specialist against the even actor on the work its ability is named for in
   its own description (`ABILITY_DEFS[].affects`: presence on nights, casting and solo briefs; stamina on whole nights;
   discipline on nights of a run and long scores; the others as now), with the two general abilities (discipline and
   stamina, `general: true`) also measured on what they're general for (growth's pace; a day's and a run's readiness).
   Not measured here, and not counted.
