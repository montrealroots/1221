## What Holds A Specialist Back (`h4/landscape-probe.ts.txt`)

On the pieces where its ability reads most, each specialist's difference from the even actor was taken apart: the gain
from its one ability at 85 alone, and the loss from the other eleven at 46.8 alone.

1. **A little of everything.** Since phase 2b every piece reads "a little of every ability its dimensions concern"
   before its own demands (`BASE_INPUTS`, `content/tasks.ts`), and the techniques at 50 take their share of every
   dimension. On its best work an ability carries 12% to 17% of what the piece's abilities move, so +35 on it gains
   about 2.7 points and −3.2 on eleven others loses about 2.45. A coin toss: ahead on about half.
2. **The weakest dimension.** The whole is three quarters the weighted mean and a quarter the weakest dimension. For an
   even performer the weakest is a three-way tie between connection, consistency and responsiveness, the three
   dimensions with no technique term, and the piece weighs it at a median 8.7% (4.6% at the tenth percentile), while it
   takes a quarter of the whole. A specialist's lowered abilities lower that dimension, and its one strength rarely
   reaches it, so a quarter of the whole can only lose. Without the term (measured, not adopted) H4 would be 9 of 12.
3. **Presence, discipline and stamina have no work of their own in the catalog.** Presence reads most in front of a
   house and with no partner, and 298 of the 431 pieces are taped briefs, most with a reader. Discipline reads in
   consistency and a long score, stamina only as a drain on the later segments. Discipline's and stamina's specialists
   are behind on every whole night (0 of 23 to 30) under every configuration measured; presence's is ahead on 5 to 11
   of 30 nights and 15 to 22 of 30 castings where the configurations leave it any, and on 1 of about 100 briefs.

### What Decides Whether A Specialist Leads

`tools/ag-baseline.ts` now writes an account of H4 at full precision (`h4Account`, its section 10): for each ability and
each piece, the ability's **share** of what the piece's abilities move (its +10 effect over the sum of the twelve's), and
whether its specialist is ahead of the even actor (by more than 0.05). On the catalog with the four new scenes
(`h4-09/ag-after-house-scenes.json`), every ability on every piece:

| The Ability's Share Of The Piece | Pairs | Its Specialist Ahead |
|---|---|---|
| under a tenth | 3,463 | **0** |
| a tenth to an eighth | 807 | 225 (28%) |
| an eighth to 15% | 386 | 381 (99%) |
| 15% or more | 564 | **564** |

So the scoring is the same rule for every ability: a specialist leads where its ability carries an eighth or more of a
piece, and never where it carries under a tenth. Where an ability is a piece's **largest input** (its own work), its
specialist leads on 10 of the 12 abilities' every such piece.

### Discipline And Stamina: Why No Content Was Added

Neither can be given whole-piece work of its own by content under the judge as built, and changing the judge for them
has no source:

- **Stamina.** A night can't be heavier than load 1 (`PIECE.load`, clamped), and eight plays are already at it; on each,
  its specialist is behind on the whole by 0.48 to 0.66 and ahead on the second half by 1.7 to 2.0. Raising the drain
  (`PIECE.drain`, SLATE's estimate, 0.3) would be a weight with no figure behind it. Its development path is the workout
  (`FITNESS`, `systems/life.ts`), which holds and builds fitness; the policy the journeys play never takes it (`l3.md`).
- **Discipline.** It's read in a scene's and a night's consistency (1 of a scene's 2 and 3 of a night's 5, once stamina is
  read as the drain), in a physical score (0.5 a level) and in a long score of units past three (`DEMAND_EFFECT.beats`), which only the
  briefs carry: a scene or a play is cut by its tones, at most three until the Dream's 5.1 made four. Its own work is the
  two long briefs it carries, both led (+1.31, +1.16); casting doesn't read it (`castingPerformer`); and its general
  effect is growth, a tenth more of what every class, call and practice day grows at 100 (`DEVELOPMENT.disciplineLift`),
  which no judged piece shows. Read for this and left out: Scapin's sack scene (Molière, The Impostures of Scapin, Act
  Three, Scene Two, tr. Charles Heron Wall, Gutenberg #8776), a long set routine of five units, every one comic. The
  catalog profiles a scene by its changes of tone, so it would be two parts like every one-tone scene, and its demands
  would be physical's and focus's (the beating, the timing); profiling it by its units instead, as a brief's beats are,
  would read it differently from every other scene. That's a change to how all 64 are read, for the owner, not one
  scene's.
